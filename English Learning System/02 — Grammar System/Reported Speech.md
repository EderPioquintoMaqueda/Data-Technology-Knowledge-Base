# 🗨️ Reported Speech | Estilo indirecto

Used to report what someone said — essential for meetings, emails and status updates.
- Direct: *Ana said, "**I am fixing** the bug."*
- Reported: *Ana said (that) **she was fixing** the bug.*

## 1. Backshift (when the reporting verb is in the past)
| Direct | Reported |
|---|---|
| Present simple — "I **work** remotely." | Past simple — she said she **worked** remotely |
| Present continuous — "I **am testing**." | Past continuous — he said he **was testing** |
| Past simple — "I **finished**." | Past perfect — she said she **had finished** |
| Present perfect — "We **have deployed**." | Past perfect — they said they **had deployed** |
| will — "I **will send** it." | would — he said he **would send** it |
| can — "I **can** help." | could — she said she **could** help |
| must — "You **must** log in." | had to — he said we **had to** log in |
| may — "It **may** fail." | might — she said it **might** fail |

💡 No backshift needed if the fact is **still true** or the reporting verb is present: *She **says** she **works** remotely.* / *He said the server **is** in Virginia.*

## 2. Time and place changes
| Direct | Reported |
|---|---|
| now | then / at that moment |
| today | that day |
| yesterday | the day before / the previous day |
| tomorrow | the next day / the following day |
| last week | the week before |
| next week | the following week |
| here | there |
| this | that |

## 3. Say vs tell
- **say** (something): *He **said** (that) it was ready.* / *He said **to me**…*
- **tell** (someone) something: *He **told me** (that) it was ready.* ❌ *He told that…* · ❌ *He said me…*

## 4. Reported questions (no inversion, no do/does!)
| Direct | Reported |
|---|---|
| "Where **is** the file?" | She asked where the file **was**. |
| "**Did** you **run** the tests?" | He asked **if/whether** I **had run** the tests. |
| "How long **will** it **take**?" | They asked how long it **would take**. |

## 5. Reported commands and requests
| Direct | Reported |
|---|---|
| "Restart the server." | He **told me to restart** the server. |
| "Please review my PR." | She **asked me to review** her PR. |
| "Don't touch production." | They **told us not to touch** production. |

## 6. Better reporting verbs (B2+)
| Verb | Pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|
| explain | that | She explained that the cache was stale. |
| suggest | -ing / that | He suggested **using** DuckDB. / He suggested that we **use** DuckDB. |
| recommend | -ing / that | They recommended **adding** an index. |
| agree | to + verb | She agreed **to lead** the project. |
| refuse | to + verb | The client refused **to pay**. |
| promise | to + verb | He promised **to send** it by Friday. |
| remind | sb to + verb | She reminded me **to update** the docs. |
| warn | sb (not) to + verb | They warned us **not to delete** the logs. |
| admit | -ing / that | He admitted **making** a mistake. |
| deny | -ing | She denied **changing** the config. |
| confirm | that | The vendor confirmed that the fix was live. |
| mention | that | Luis mentioned that the API was slow. |

## 7. Status-update example
> *In yesterday's stand-up, Carlos **said** he **had finished** the migration script and **was** now **testing** it. Ana **asked whether** the old tables **would be** archived, and Carlos **explained** that they **would be kept** for 30 days. The manager **reminded** everyone **to update** their tickets.*

## 🧪 Practice
1. "I am working on the dashboard." → She said… 2. "Will the API be ready?" → He asked… 3. "Don't push to main." → The lead told us… 4. "We deployed the model yesterday." → They said…
<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. She said she was working on the dashboard. 2. He asked if the API would be ready. 3. The lead told us not to push to main. 4. They said they had deployed the model the day before.
</details>
