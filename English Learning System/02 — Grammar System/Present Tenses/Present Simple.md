# Present Simple

## 1. What is the Present Simple?

El **Present Simple** es uno de los tiempos verbales fundamentales del inglés. Se utiliza para hablar de acciones habituales, rutinas, hechos generales, situaciones permanentes, preferencias y estados.

En español suele corresponder al presente del indicativo:

|English|Español|
|---|---|
|I work|Yo trabajo|
|You study|Tú estudias|
|She lives|Ella vive|
|We need|Nosotros necesitamos|
|They develop software|Ellos desarrollan software|

Sin embargo, no debes asumir que todas las oraciones en presente español se traducen utilizando Present Simple. En inglés también existe el **Present Continuous**, que expresa acciones que están ocurriendo o situaciones temporales.

Examples:

- I work in administration. — Trabajo en administración.
    
- I am working right now. — Estoy trabajando ahora mismo.
    

La diferencia entre ambos tiempos se estudiará en el documento [[Present Continuous]].

## 2. When do we use the Present Simple?

El Present Simple tiene varios usos. Es importante comprender la intención de cada uno para elegir correctamente el tiempo verbal.

### 2.1. Habits and routines

Se utiliza para describir actividades que realizamos habitualmente.

Examples:

- I wake up at six every morning.
    
- She goes to work by car.
    
- We have meetings every Monday.
    
- They study English after work.
    
- He checks his email every morning.
    

Expresiones frecuentes:

- every day — todos los días
    
- every morning — todas las mañanas
    
- every week — cada semana
    
- every month — cada mes
    
- usually — usualmente
    
- always — siempre
    
- often — frecuentemente
    
- sometimes — a veces
    
- rarely — rara vez
    
- never — nunca
    

Professional examples:

- I review technical documents every day.
    
- The department prepares monthly reports.
    
- The engineers inspect the machines regularly.
    
- The team updates the database every week.
    

### 2.2. General facts and truths

Se utiliza para expresar hechos generales, científicos o situaciones consideradas verdaderas.

Examples:

- Water boils at 100°C at standard atmospheric pressure.
    
- The Earth revolves around the Sun.
    
- Computers process information.
    
- Metal expands when heated.
    
- Plants need water to grow.
    

Professional and technical examples:

- A database stores structured information.
    
- Python supports different programming paradigms.
    
- An electric motor converts electrical energy into mechanical energy.
    
- A server receives and processes requests.
    

### 2.3. Permanent or stable situations

Se utiliza para describir situaciones que se consideran estables o permanentes, aunque no necesariamente sean definitivas.

Examples:

- I live in Mexico.
    
- She works for a technology company.
    
- They own a manufacturing business.
    
- He speaks Spanish and English.
    
- My brother studies engineering.
    

Professional examples:

- The company operates in several countries.
    
- The department manages the vehicle inventory.
    
- The system stores information in PostgreSQL.
    

### 2.4. Preferences and opinions

Se utiliza con frecuencia para expresar gustos, preferencias, pensamientos y opiniones.

Examples:

- I like technology.
    
- She loves music.
    
- They prefer working remotely.
    
- I believe this solution works.
    
- We need more information.
    

Verbs commonly used in this context:

|Verb|Español|
|---|---|
|like|gustar|
|love|encantar|
|prefer|preferir|
|hate|odiar|
|want|querer|
|need|necesitar|
|know|saber / conocer|
|understand|entender|
|believe|creer|
|think|pensar|
|remember|recordar|
|recognize|reconocer|

Algunos de estos verbos describen estados mentales o preferencias y normalmente se utilizan en Present Simple cuando expresan una condición o estado, no una acción en desarrollo.

### 2.5. Schedules and timetables

Se utiliza para hablar de horarios establecidos, calendarios y programas.

Examples:

- The train leaves at 8:30.
    
- The meeting starts at nine.
    
- The office opens at 8:00 a.m.
    
- The course ends in December.
    
- The flight arrives at 6:45 p.m.
    

Este uso también puede referirse a acontecimientos futuros programados.

## 3. Present Simple: sentence structure

La estructura básica del Present Simple depende del sujeto.

Existen dos grupos principales:

1. Primera y segunda persona, además de la tercera persona plural: `I`, `you`, `we`, `they`.
    
2. Tercera persona singular: `he`, `she`, `it`.
    

La diferencia principal está en la forma del verbo y en el uso de los auxiliares `do` y `does`.

### 3.1. Affirmative sentences

**Structure:**

`Subject + Base Verb + Complement`

Con `I`, `you`, `we` y `they`, utilizamos el verbo en su forma base.

|Subject|Verb|Complement|
|---|---|---|
|I|work|in an office.|
|You|study|English.|
|We|develop|software.|
|They|manage|projects.|

Examples:

- I work in administration.
    
- You speak English.
    
- We study programming.
    
- They develop applications.
    

Con `he`, `she` e `it`, el verbo normalmente agrega `-s` o `-es`.

|Subject|Verb|Complement|
|---|---|---|
|He|works|in an office.|
|She|studies|English.|
|It|operates|automatically.|

Examples:

- He works in engineering.
    
- She manages the department.
    
- The system stores information.
    
- The application processes data.
    

**Regla fundamental:** en una oración afirmativa, la tercera persona singular modifica el verbo principal.

### 3.2. Negative sentences

Para construir oraciones negativas utilizamos los auxiliares:

- `do not` / `don't`
    
- `does not` / `doesn't`
    

**Structure:**

`Subject + Do/Does + Not + Base Verb + Complement`

|Subject|Auxiliary|Example|
|---|---|---|
|I|do not|I do not work here.|
|You|do not|You do not understand.|
|We|do not|We do not need this.|
|They|do not|They do not use Python.|
|He|does not|He does not work here.|
|She|does not|She does not drive.|
|It|does not|It does not function correctly.|

Examples:

- I don't speak French.
    
- We don't have enough time.
    
- They don't work on Sundays.
    
- He doesn't understand the instructions.
    
- She doesn't use that application.
    
- The system doesn't store personal information.
    

**Important:** cuando utilizamos `doesn't`, el verbo principal regresa a su forma base.

Incorrect:

- She doesn't works here.
    
- He doesn't studies English.
    
- The system doesn't stores information.
    

Correct:

- She doesn't work here.
    
- He doesn't study English.
    
- The system doesn't store information.
    

La terminación de tercera persona ya está expresada mediante `does`.

### 3.3. Interrogative sentences

Para formular preguntas utilizamos `do` o `does` al inicio.

**Structure:**

`Do/Does + Subject + Base Verb + Complement + ?`

Examples:

|Auxiliary|Subject|Verb|Question|
|---|---|---|---|
|Do|you|work|Do you work here?|
|Do|they|study|Do they study English?|
|Do|we|need|Do we need this?|
|Does|he|work|Does he work here?|
|Does|she|speak|Does she speak English?|
|Does|it|work|Does it work correctly?|

Examples:

- Do you speak English?
    
- Do they work remotely?
    
- Does she know the answer?
    
- Does he manage the project?
    
- Does the application support PostgreSQL?
    

**Important:** después de `does`, el verbo principal siempre utiliza su forma base.

Incorrect:

- Does she works here?
    
- Does he speaks English?
    
- Does it functions correctly?
    

Correct:

- Does she work here?
    
- Does he speak English?
    
- Does it function correctly?
    

### 3.4. Short answers

Las preguntas con `do` y `does` normalmente pueden responderse de manera breve.

**Structure:**

`Yes, + Subject + do/does.`

`No, + Subject + don't/doesn't.`

|Question|Affirmative|Negative|
|---|---|---|
|Do you work here?|Yes, I do.|No, I don't.|
|Do they speak English?|Yes, they do.|No, they don't.|
|Does she study?|Yes, she does.|No, she doesn't.|
|Does he drive?|Yes, he does.|No, he doesn't.|
|Does it work?|Yes, it does.|No, it doesn't.|

No es necesario repetir el verbo principal en una short answer.

Incorrect:

- Yes, I work.
    
- No, she not.
    

Correct:

- Yes, I do.
    
- No, she doesn't.
    

Las respuestas completas también son correctas, pero las respuestas cortas son muy frecuentes en conversaciones cotidianas.

## 4. Third-person singular: he, she, it

Una de las reglas más importantes del Present Simple es la modificación del verbo cuando el sujeto está en tercera persona singular.

Esto incluye:

- He — él.
    
- She — ella.
    
- It — eso / ello.
    
- Un nombre propio singular: John, Maria.
    
- Un sustantivo singular: the company, the system, my brother.
    

### 4.1. General rule: add -s

La regla general consiste en agregar `-s`.

|Base verb|Third-person singular|
|---|---|
|work|works|
|read|reads|
|write|writes|
|speak|speaks|
|develop|develops|
|manage|manages|
|need|needs|
|want|wants|
|know|knows|
|understand|understands|
|run|runs|
|open|opens|

Examples:

- She works in technology.
    
- He reads technical documentation.
    
- The company develops software.
    
- The system stores records.
    

### 4.2. Verbs ending in -ch, -sh, -ss, -x, -z and -o

Generalmente agregamos `-es`.

|Base verb|Third-person singular|
|---|---|
|watch|watches|
|teach|teaches|
|wash|washes|
|finish|finishes|
|pass|passes|
|discuss|discusses|
|fix|fixes|
|mix|mixes|
|go|goes|
|do|does|

Examples:

- She watches technical videos.
    
- He teaches mathematics.
    
- The application fixes certain errors.
    
- The employee finishes the report.
    
- The company goes through several changes.
    

### 4.3. Consonant + y → -ies

Cuando un verbo termina en consonante + `y`, cambiamos la `y` por `ies`.

|Base verb|Third-person singular|
|---|---|
|study|studies|
|try|tries|
|carry|carries|
|apply|applies|
|identify|identifies|
|modify|modifies|
|verify|verifies|
|classify|classifies|

Examples:

- She studies English.
    
- He applies the procedure.
    
- The system identifies errors.
    
- The software verifies the information.
    

### 4.4. Vowel + y → -s

Cuando antes de `y` hay una vocal, solamente agregamos `-s`.

|Base verb|Third-person singular|
|---|---|
|play|plays|
|enjoy|enjoys|
|buy|buys|
|say|says|
|stay|stays|

Examples:

- He plays football.
    
- She enjoys reading.
    
- The company buys new equipment.
    

### 4.5. Irregular verbs: have and be

Dos verbos requieren atención especial.

|Subject|Have|Be|
|---|---|---|
|I|have|am|
|You|have|are|
|He|has|is|
|She|has|is|
|It|has|is|
|We|have|are|
|They|have|are|

Examples:

- She has a computer.
    
- He has experience in engineering.
    
- The system has several modules.
    
- The company is growing.
    
- They are employees.
    

**Nota:** el verbo `be` no utiliza los auxiliares `do` y `does` para formar negativas y preguntas. Tiene su propia estructura, que se estudió en [[Sentence Structure]].

## 5. The auxiliary verbs: DO and DOES

Los auxiliares `do` y `does` son fundamentales para construir preguntas y negativas en Present Simple.

No suelen tener una traducción directa en estas estructuras. Su función es gramatical: permiten identificar que estamos formulando una pregunta o una negación.

### 5.1. When do we use DO?

Utilizamos `do` con:

- I
    
- You
    
- We
    
- They
    

Examples:

- Do you work here?
    
- I don't understand.
    
- Do they need help?
    
- We don't use that software.
    

### 5.2. When do we use DOES?

Utilizamos `does` con:

- He
    
- She
    
- It
    
- Un sujeto singular.
    

Examples:

- Does he work here?
    
- She doesn't understand.
    
- Does the company need more employees?
    
- The system doesn't recognize the password.
    

### 5.3. DO as an auxiliary and as a main verb

`Do` puede cumplir dos funciones:

1. Auxiliary verb: ayuda a construir preguntas y negativas.
    
2. Main verb: significa hacer o realizar.
    

Examples:

- Do you work here? — Auxiliar.
    
- I don't work here. — Auxiliar.
    
- I do my homework. — Verbo principal.
    
- She does her job well. — Verbo principal.
    

Observa este ejemplo:

- Does she do her homework?
    

El primer `does` es un auxiliar y el segundo `do` es el verbo principal.

Otro ejemplo:

- He doesn't do the maintenance work.
    

`Doesn't` forma la negación y `do` expresa la acción de realizar.

## 6. WH-questions in Present Simple

Las **WH-questions** son preguntas que solicitan información específica.

Se construyen utilizando palabras interrogativas como:

|Question word|Español|
|---|---|
|What|Qué / cuál|
|Where|Dónde|
|When|Cuándo|
|Why|Por qué|
|Who|Quién|
|Whom|A quién|
|Which|Cuál / cuáles|
|Whose|De quién|
|How|Cómo|
|How often|Con qué frecuencia|
|How much|Cuánto|
|How many|Cuántos|
|How long|Cuánto tiempo|
|How far|Qué tan lejos|

### 6.1. Structure

**Structure:**

`WH-word + Do/Does + Subject + Base Verb + Complement + ?`

Examples:

|Question|Español|
|---|---|
|What do you do?|¿A qué te dedicas?|
|Where do you work?|¿Dónde trabajas?|
|When do you study?|¿Cuándo estudias?|
|Why do you need this?|¿Por qué necesitas esto?|
|How do you use Python?|¿Cómo utilizas Python?|
|How often do you exercise?|¿Con qué frecuencia haces ejercicio?|
|Where does she live?|¿Dónde vive ella?|
|What does the system do?|¿Qué hace el sistema?|
|Why does he study English?|¿Por qué estudia inglés?|

### 6.2. What do you do?

Esta pregunta es especialmente importante en contextos profesionales.

`What do you do?` significa normalmente:

- ¿A qué te dedicas?
    
- ¿Cuál es tu profesión?
    
- ¿En qué trabajas?
    

No debe confundirse con:

`What are you doing?`

Esta segunda pregunta significa:

- ¿Qué estás haciendo?
    

Examples:

- What do you do? — I work in engineering.
    
- What are you doing? — I'm studying English.
    

La primera utiliza Present Simple y la segunda Present Continuous.

### 6.3. Questions with WHO as subject

Existe una excepción importante cuando `who` funciona como sujeto de la pregunta.

En este caso normalmente no utilizamos `do` o `does`.

Compare:

- Who works here? — ¿Quién trabaja aquí?
    
- Who does she work with? — ¿Con quién trabaja ella?
    

En la primera pregunta, `who` es el sujeto que realiza la acción.

En la segunda, `she` es el sujeto y `who` pregunta por la persona con la que trabaja.

Other examples:

- Who manages the department?
    
- Who develops the application?
    
- Who uses this database?
    

## 7. Adverbs of frequency

Los **adverbs of frequency** expresan con qué frecuencia sucede una actividad.

Son especialmente comunes en Present Simple porque este tiempo se utiliza para describir rutinas y hábitos.

### 7.1. Common adverbs

|Adverb|Meaning|Approximate frequency|
|---|---|---|
|Always|Siempre|100%|
|Usually|Usualmente|80–90%|
|Normally|Normalmente|80–90%|
|Frequently|Frecuentemente|70–80%|
|Often|A menudo|60–70%|
|Sometimes|A veces|40–50%|
|Occasionally|Ocasionalmente|20–30%|
|Rarely|Rara vez|5–10%|
|Seldom|Casi nunca|5–10%|
|Hardly ever|Casi nunca|1–5%|
|Never|Nunca|0%|

Los porcentajes son orientativos, no valores matemáticos exactos.

### 7.2. Position with ordinary verbs

Normalmente, los adverbios de frecuencia se colocan antes del verbo principal.

**Structure:**

`Subject + Adverb of Frequency + Main Verb + Complement`

Examples:

- I always study English.
    
- She usually arrives early.
    
- We often use Excel.
    
- They sometimes work remotely.
    
- He rarely drives to work.
    
- I never drink alcohol.
    

### 7.3. Position with verb TO BE

Cuando utilizamos el verbo `to be`, el adverbio generalmente aparece después del verbo.

**Structure:**

`Subject + Verb TO BE + Adverb of Frequency + Complement`

Examples:

- I am always busy.
    
- She is usually punctual.
    
- They are often tired.
    
- He is never late.
    

Compare:

- I usually work at home.
    
- I am usually at home.
    

En la primera oración, `usually` aparece antes del verbo principal `work`.

En la segunda, aparece después de `am`.

### 7.4. Frequency expressions

También podemos utilizar expresiones que indican frecuencia.

|Expression|Español|
|---|---|
|every day|todos los días|
|every week|cada semana|
|once a day|una vez al día|
|twice a week|dos veces por semana|
|three times a month|tres veces al mes|
|on Mondays|los lunes|
|on weekends|los fines de semana|
|from time to time|de vez en cuando|

Examples:

- I study English every day.
    
- We have meetings twice a week.
    
- She exercises three times a week.
    
- The company reviews its procedures once a year.
    

Generalmente estas expresiones se colocan al final de la oración.

## 8. Stative verbs

Los **stative verbs** describen estados, condiciones, pensamientos, emociones, posesión o percepción, en lugar de acciones físicas en desarrollo.

Muchos de estos verbos suelen utilizarse en Present Simple.

### 8.1. Mental states

|Verb|Español|
|---|---|
|know|saber / conocer|
|understand|entender|
|believe|creer|
|remember|recordar|
|forget|olvidar|
|recognize|reconocer|
|think|pensar / opinar|

Examples:

- I know the answer.
    
- She understands the process.
    
- They believe the solution is correct.
    
- He remembers the instructions.
    

### 8.2. Emotions and preferences

|Verb|Español|
|---|---|
|love|amar / encantar|
|like|gustar|
|dislike|no gustar|
|hate|odiar|
|prefer|preferir|
|want|querer|
|need|necesitar|

Examples:

- I like technology.
    
- She prefers working remotely.
    
- We need more information.
    
- He wants a new computer.
    

### 8.3. Possession

|Verb|Español|
|---|---|
|have|tener|
|own|poseer|
|belong|pertenecer|
|possess|poseer|

Examples:

- I have a computer.
    
- She owns a small business.
    
- This equipment belongs to the company.
    

### 8.4. Senses and perception

|Verb|Español|
|---|---|
|see|ver|
|hear|oír|
|smell|oler|
|taste|saber / tener sabor|
|feel|sentir|
|seem|parecer|
|appear|parecer|

Examples:

- I see the problem.
    
- This solution seems effective.
    
- The food tastes good.
    
- The machine sounds unusual.
    

### 8.5. Important exceptions

Algunos verbos pueden ser stative o dynamic dependiendo de su significado.

**Think**

- I think this is a good idea. — Opinar.
    
- I am thinking about the problem. — Reflexionar en este momento.
    

**Have**

- I have a car. — Posesión.
    
- I am having lunch. — Acción en desarrollo.
    

**See**

- I see what you mean. — Comprender.
    
- I am seeing my manager tomorrow. — Tener una cita o reunión.
    

**Taste**

- The food tastes good. — Tener sabor.
    
- The chef is tasting the food. — Probar algo.
    

No todos los stative verbs están prohibidos en Present Continuous; el significado determina el uso.

## 9. Present Simple with BE vs. Present Simple with DO/DOES

Es importante distinguir entre las estructuras que utilizan el verbo `to be` y las que utilizan otros verbos principales.

### 9.1. With TO BE

|Type|Structure|Example|
|---|---|---|
|Affirmative|Subject + am/is/are|She is an engineer.|
|Negative|Subject + am/is/are + not|She is not an engineer.|
|Interrogative|Am/Is/Are + Subject?|Is she an engineer?|

### 9.2. With ordinary verbs

|Type|Structure|Example|
|---|---|---|
|Affirmative|Subject + verb|She works here.|
|Negative|Subject + don't/doesn't + verb|She doesn't work here.|
|Interrogative|Do/Does + subject + verb?|Does she work here?|

Compare:

- Is she an engineer?
    
- Does she work as an engineer?
    

La primera pregunta se refiere a su identidad o profesión.

La segunda pregunta se refiere a su actividad laboral.

**Important:** no utilizamos `do` o `does` para formar preguntas y negativas con `to be`.

Incorrect:

- Does she be an engineer?
    
- She doesn't be an engineer.
    

Correct:

- Is she an engineer?
    
- She isn't an engineer.
    

## 10. Present Simple in professional contexts

El Present Simple es fundamental para describir responsabilidades, procesos, funciones, servicios y actividades dentro de una empresa.

### 10.1. Describing job responsibilities

Examples:

- I manage technical documentation.
    
- I prepare monthly reports.
    
- I review maintenance records.
    
- I coordinate operational activities.
    
- I analyze information using Excel.
    
- I develop software applications.
    

### 10.2. Describing a company

Examples:

- The company provides technology services.
    
- The organization operates in several countries.
    
- The department manages administrative processes.
    
- The team develops digital solutions.
    
- The business serves corporate clients.
    

### 10.3. Describing a software application

Examples:

- The application connects to a PostgreSQL database.
    
- The system stores vehicle information.
    
- Users search for specific records.
    
- The platform generates operational reports.
    
- The application validates user input.
    
- The database contains approximately 200 vehicle records.
    

### 10.4. Explaining a technical process

Examples:

- The sensor measures temperature.
    
- The controller receives the signal.
    
- The motor converts electrical energy into mechanical energy.
    
- The system processes the information.
    
- The application displays the results.
    

Estas estructuras son útiles para entrevistas, presentaciones técnicas y documentación profesional.

## 12. Common mistakes made by Spanish speakers

|Incorrect|Correct|Explanation|
|---|---|---|
|She work here.|She works here.|Tercera persona singular.|
|He study English.|He studies English.|Consonant + y → ies.|
|She don't work.|She doesn't work.|Utilizar does con she.|
|He doesn't works.|He doesn't work.|Verbo en forma base después de doesn't.|
|Does she works?|Does she work?|Verbo en forma base después de does.|
|Do he work?|Does he work?|He utiliza does.|
|I am work here.|I work here.|No utilizar am antes del verbo principal en Present Simple.|
|I no understand.|I don't understand.|La negación requiere auxiliar.|
|She is work in IT.|She works in IT.|No confundir to be con verbo principal.|
|He go to work.|He goes to work.|Go → goes.|
|The company have a database.|The company has a database.|Have → has.|
|Does it works?|Does it work?|El auxiliar lleva la marca de tercera persona.|

## 13. Present Simple contractions

Las **contractions** son formas abreviadas utilizadas principalmente en conversaciones y comunicación informal.

|Full form|Contraction|
|---|---|
|do not|don't|
|does not|doesn't|

Examples:

- I do not work here. → I don't work here.
    
- They do not understand. → They don't understand.
    
- She does not drive. → She doesn't drive.
    
- The system does not work. → The system doesn't work.
    

En documentos técnicos, académicos o formales, puede ser preferible utilizar las formas completas, dependiendo del estilo del documento.

En conversación cotidiana, las contracciones son muy comunes.

## 14. Present Simple: complete structure reference

|Sentence type|I / You / We / They|He / She / It|
|---|---|---|
|Affirmative|I work.|She works.|
|Negative|I don't work.|She doesn't work.|
|Interrogative|Do you work?|Does she work?|
|Short answer: Yes|Yes, I do.|Yes, she does.|
|Short answer: No|No, I don't.|No, she doesn't.|

### General formula

**Affirmative**

`Subject + Base Verb (+ s/es for he/she/it) + Complement`

**Negative**

`Subject + do/does + not + Base Verb + Complement`

**Interrogative**

`Do/Does + Subject + Base Verb + Complement + ?`

**WH-question**

`WH-word + do/does + Subject + Base Verb + Complement + ?`

## 16. Final concepts to remember

1. Present Simple expresa hábitos, rutinas, hechos generales, estados y situaciones estables.
    
2. Con `I`, `you`, `we` y `they`, utilizamos el verbo en su forma base.
    
3. Con `he`, `she` e `it`, agregamos normalmente `-s` o `-es` en afirmativo.
    
4. Para formar negativas utilizamos `don't` o `doesn't`.
    
5. Para formular preguntas utilizamos `do` o `does` al inicio.
    
6. Después de `does` y `doesn't`, el verbo principal siempre aparece en su forma base.
    
7. Los adverbios de frecuencia permiten expresar qué tan habitual es una actividad.
    
8. Los stative verbs describen estados y suelen utilizarse en Present Simple.
    
9. El verbo `to be` tiene una estructura propia y no utiliza `do/does` para formar preguntas o negativas.
    
10. Este tiempo es esencial para explicar responsabilidades, procesos y actividades profesionales.
