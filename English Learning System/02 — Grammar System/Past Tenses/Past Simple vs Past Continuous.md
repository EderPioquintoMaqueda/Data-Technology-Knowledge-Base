# Past Simple vs Past Continuous

## 1. Introduction

Past Simple y Past Continuous permiten hablar de acontecimientos anteriores, pero describen las acciones desde perspectivas diferentes.

La distinción principal es:

- **Past Simple:** presenta una acción o acontecimiento como un hecho pasado, generalmente terminado.
    
- **Past Continuous:** presenta una acción como algo que estaba desarrollándose en un momento del pasado.
    

Observa:

|Past Simple|Past Continuous|
|---|---|
|I worked yesterday.|I was working yesterday at 10 a.m.|
|Trabajé ayer.|Ayer a las 10 estaba trabajando.|
|She called me.|She was calling me.|
|Ella me llamó.|Ella estaba llamándome.|
|They repaired the machine.|They were repairing the machine.|
|Repararon la máquina.|Estaban reparando la máquina.|

La diferencia no consiste simplemente en que una acción sea corta y la otra larga. Lo importante es cómo se presenta el acontecimiento:

- Como un hecho o evento: Past Simple.
    
- Como una actividad en desarrollo: Past Continuous.
    

Una acción puede durar varias horas y utilizar Past Simple si se presenta como terminada:

- I worked for eight hours yesterday.
    

También puede durar unos pocos minutos y utilizar Past Continuous si se observa mientras estaba ocurriendo:

- I was talking to my manager when you arrived.
    

---

## 2. The fundamental concept: completed event vs action in progress

### 2.1 Past Simple: completed events

Utilizamos Past Simple para presentar acontecimientos como hechos del pasado.

Ejemplos:

- I arrived at the office.
    
- She opened the application.
    
- We completed the report.
    
- The machine stopped.
    
- The manager called the technical team.
    

Cada oración presenta un acontecimiento o una acción como un hecho ocurrido.

### 2.2 Past Continuous: actions in progress

Utilizamos Past Continuous para describir qué estaba sucediendo en un momento determinado.

Ejemplos:

- I was driving to the office.
    
- She was opening the application.
    
- We were preparing the report.
    
- The machine was operating.
    
- The manager was talking to the technical team.
    

El énfasis está en el desarrollo de la actividad.

### 2.3 Understanding the difference through context

Compara estas dos oraciones:

**I repaired the machine.**

Reparé la máquina. Presento la reparación como una actividad realizada y terminada.

**I was repairing the machine.**

Estaba reparando la máquina. Presento la actividad como algo que se encontraba en desarrollo.

La segunda oración no informa necesariamente si terminé la reparación.

Esta distinción es importante: Past Continuous no significa automáticamente que la acción nunca se completó.

---

## 3. Interrupted actions: when and while

Uno de los usos más importantes de ambos tiempos consiste en describir una acción que estaba desarrollándose cuando ocurrió otro acontecimiento.

Ejemplo:

**I was working when the phone rang.**

Estaba trabajando cuando sonó el teléfono.

La estructura representa dos acontecimientos:

1. Trabajar era una actividad en desarrollo.
    
2. El teléfono sonó durante esa actividad.
    

### 3.1 Typical structure with WHEN

**Past Continuous + when + Past Simple**

Ejemplos:

- I was studying when my friend called.
    
- She was driving when the accident happened.
    
- We were testing the system when it crashed.
    
- They were repairing the machine when the power went out.
    
- I was preparing the report when my manager arrived.
    

También podemos invertir el orden:

**When + Past Simple, + Past Continuous**

- When my friend called, I was studying.
    
- When the accident happened, she was driving.
    
- When the system crashed, we were testing it.
    
- When the power went out, they were repairing the machine.
    

Ambas estructuras son correctas.

### 3.2 The role of WHEN

_When_ significa cuando y permite introducir un acontecimiento o momento que coincide con otra actividad.

Ejemplos:

- I was sleeping when you called.
    
- The computer stopped working when I opened the application.
    
- The manager arrived when we were discussing the project.
    

En los últimos ejemplos, el orden de los tiempos depende de qué acción se presenta como acontecimiento y cuál como actividad en desarrollo.

---

## 4. Simultaneous actions: WHILE

_While_ significa mientras y permite relacionar actividades que estaban sucediendo al mismo tiempo.

### 4.1 Two actions in progress

Estructura frecuente:

**Past Continuous + while + Past Continuous**

Ejemplos:

- I was studying while my brother was watching television.
    
- She was working while he was preparing dinner.
    
- We were analyzing the data while the system was processing information.
    
- The engineers were inspecting the equipment while the technicians were documenting the results.
    

Ambas actividades estaban en desarrollo.

### 4.2 WHILE with Past Simple

También podemos utilizar _while_ cuando una actividad en desarrollo coincide con un acontecimiento puntual.

- While I was working, the phone rang.
    
- While she was driving, the accident happened.
    
- While we were testing the application, the system crashed.
    
- While the engineers were inspecting the equipment, the alarm activated.
    

En estos ejemplos:

- La actividad en desarrollo utiliza Past Continuous.
    
- El acontecimiento utiliza Past Simple.
    

### 4.3 WHEN vs WHILE

|WHEN|WHILE|
|---|---|
|Cuando|Mientras|
|Introduce un momento o acontecimiento.|Destaca la duración o simultaneidad.|
|I was working when he arrived.|I was working while she was studying.|
|Estaba trabajando cuando llegó.|Estaba trabajando mientras ella estudiaba.|

No son palabras intercambiables en todas las situaciones. La elección depende de la relación temporal que queremos comunicar.

## 5. Sequence of events in the past

Past Simple permite narrar varios acontecimientos que ocurrieron uno después de otro.

Ejemplo:

- I arrived at the office.
    
- I checked my emails.
    
- I attended a meeting.
    
- I prepared a report.
    
- I finished my work.
    

Todos los acontecimientos utilizan Past Simple porque se presentan como acciones realizadas.

### 5.1 Combining background and events

Cuando contamos una historia, podemos utilizar Past Continuous para describir el escenario y Past Simple para presentar los acontecimientos.

Ejemplo:

**The engineers were working in the factory when the machine stopped.**

Los ingenieros estaban trabajando en la fábrica cuando la máquina se detuvo.

- _Were working:_ actividad en desarrollo.
    
- _Stopped:_ acontecimiento que ocurrió durante esa actividad.
    

Otro ejemplo:

**I was walking home when I saw an accident.**

Estaba caminando a casa cuando vi un accidente.

- _Was walking:_ contexto o actividad en desarrollo.
    
- _Saw:_ acontecimiento.
    

### 5.2 A longer narrative

**English:**

Yesterday, I was working on a technical project. The engineering team was testing a machine when the system suddenly stopped. One technician checked the electrical connections while another engineer reviewed the control system. We identified the problem and replaced the damaged component. After that, the machine started working again.

**Español:**

Ayer estaba trabajando en un proyecto técnico. El equipo de ingeniería estaba probando una máquina cuando el sistema se detuvo repentinamente. Un técnico revisó las conexiones eléctricas mientras otro ingeniero examinaba el sistema de control. Identificamos el problema y reemplazamos el componente dañado. Después de eso, la máquina comenzó a funcionar nuevamente.

Observa la distribución:

|Sentence|Tense|Function|
|---|---|---|
|I was working on a technical project.|Past Continuous|Contexto|
|The team was testing a machine.|Past Continuous|Actividad en desarrollo|
|The system suddenly stopped.|Past Simple|Acontecimiento|
|One technician checked the connections.|Past Simple|Acción realizada|
|Another engineer reviewed the system.|Past Simple|Acción realizada|
|We identified the problem.|Past Simple|Resultado|
|We replaced the component.|Past Simple|Acción terminada|

## 6. Verbs that change the meaning depending on the tense

El mismo verbo puede transmitir información diferente dependiendo de si utilizamos Past Simple o Past Continuous.

### 6.1 Work

- I worked yesterday. = Trabajé ayer.
    
- I was working at 9 a.m. = Estaba trabajando a las 9 a. m.
    

La primera presenta el trabajo como un hecho. La segunda describe qué estaba sucediendo a esa hora.

### 6.2 Read

- She read the report. = Ella leyó el informe.
    
- She was reading the report. = Ella estaba leyendo el informe.
    

La primera presenta la lectura como un acontecimiento. La segunda la muestra en desarrollo.

### 6.3 Write

- I wrote an email. = Escribí un correo.
    
- I was writing an email when you called. = Estaba escribiendo un correo cuando llamaste.
    

### 6.4 Develop

- We developed an application. = Desarrollamos una aplicación.
    
- We were developing an application when the project changed. = Estábamos desarrollando una aplicación cuando cambió el proyecto.
    

### 6.5 Build

- They built a new database. = Construyeron una nueva base de datos.
    
- They were building a new database when the requirements changed. = Estaban construyendo una nueva base de datos cuando cambiaron los requisitos.
    

### 6.6 Drive

- He drove to the office. = Condujo hasta la oficina.
    
- He was driving when it started raining. = Estaba conduciendo cuando comenzó a llover.
    

### 6.7 Meet

- I met the manager yesterday. = Conocí al gerente ayer.
    
- I was meeting the manager when you arrived. = Estaba reuniéndome con el gerente cuando llegaste.

## 7. Stative verbs: an important exception

Los verbos de estado (_stative verbs_) normalmente no se utilizan en tiempos Continuous, incluso cuando hablamos del pasado.

Ejemplos:

|Stative verb|Correct Past Simple|
|---|---|
|Know|I knew the answer.|
|Believe|She believed him.|
|Understand|We understood the problem.|
|Need|They needed help.|
|Want|I wanted a new computer.|
|Own|He owned the company.|
|Prefer|She preferred Python.|
|Remember|I remembered his name.|

Incorrect: I was knowing the answer.

Correct: I knew the answer.

Sin embargo, algunos verbos cambian de significado y pueden utilizarse en Continuous cuando expresan una actividad dinámica.

|Verb|Past Simple|Past Continuous|
|---|---|---|
|Think|I thought it was correct.|I was thinking about the problem.|
|Have|I had a computer.|I was having lunch.|
|See|I saw the problem.|I was seeing a client.|
|Feel|I felt tired.|I was feeling the material.|
|Look|She looked tired.|She was looking at the screen.|

La interpretación depende del significado específico del verbo.

## 8. Time expressions and their relationship with both tenses

Las expresiones temporales ayudan a situar las acciones, pero no determinan por sí solas el tiempo verbal.

### 8.1 Expressions commonly used with Past Simple

|Expression|Meaning|
|---|---|
|Yesterday|Ayer|
|Last week|La semana pasada|
|Last month|El mes pasado|
|Last year|El año pasado|
|Two days ago|Hace dos días|
|In 2024|En 2024|
|Earlier|Antes|
|Suddenly|Repentinamente|
|Then|Después / entonces|
|Finally|Finalmente|

Ejemplos:

- I finished the report yesterday.
    
- She called me two hours ago.
    
- We completed the project last month.
    
- The system suddenly stopped.
    

### 8.2 Expressions commonly used with Past Continuous

|Expression|Meaning|
|---|---|
|At that moment|En ese momento|
|At that time|En ese momento|
|At 8 a.m. yesterday|Ayer a las 8 a. m.|
|While|Mientras|
|All morning|Toda la mañana|
|During the meeting|Durante la reunión|

Ejemplos:

- I was working at that moment.
    
- They were testing the equipment at 8 a.m.
    
- We were discussing the project during the meeting.
    

### 8.3 Same expression, different tense

Una expresión puede acompañar ambos tiempos.

**Yesterday:**

- I worked yesterday. = Trabajé ayer.
    
- I was working yesterday at 10 a.m. = Ayer a las 10 estaba trabajando.
    

**At 8 p.m.:**

- I finished the report at 8 p.m. = Terminé el informe a las 8 p. m.
    
- I was working at 8 p.m. = Estaba trabajando a las 8 p. m.
    

El contexto y la intención determinan el tiempo verbal.

|Incorrect|Correct|Explanation|
|---|---|---|
|I was worked when he arrived.|I was working when he arrived.|Past Continuous requiere -ing.|
|I working when he called.|I was working when he called.|Falta el auxiliar.|
|When I was arrived, she was working.|When I arrived, she was working.|Arrived es el acontecimiento puntual.|
|While I worked, he was studying.|While I was working, he was studying.|La segunda estructura destaca dos actividades en desarrollo.|
|She were studying when I called.|She was studying when I called.|She utiliza was.|
|Did you were working?|Were you working?|La pregunta utiliza was/were.|
|I was knowing the answer.|I knew the answer.|Know normalmente es stative.|
|They was repairing the machine.|They were repairing the machine.|They utiliza were.|
|The machine was stop.|The machine stopped.|Se necesita la forma correcta del verbo.|

## 10. Professional English: explaining what happened at work

Esta distinción es especialmente útil para explicar problemas, proyectos, reuniones y experiencias laborales.

### 10.1 Technical incidents

**Question: What happened yesterday?**

- The machine stopped during operation.
    
- The technicians were inspecting the equipment.
    
- The system generated an error.
    
- We identified the problem and replaced the component.
    

### 10.2 Software development

**Question: What were you working on when the error occurred?**

- I was developing a database application.
    
- We were testing a new function.
    
- The system was processing information.
    
- The application stopped responding while we were testing it.
    

### 10.3 Data analysis

**Question: What happened while you were analyzing the data?**

- I was reviewing the dataset.
    
- The application generated an error.
    
- I checked the records and identified an inconsistency.
    
- I corrected the information and generated the report again.
    

### 10.4 Job interview storytelling

Una entrevista puede solicitar que expliques una situación anterior.

**Question: Tell me about a technical problem you solved.**

**Example answer:**

I was working on an internal application when I noticed a problem with the database. The system was storing incorrect information, so I reviewed the records and identified the source of the error. I corrected the data and tested the application again. Finally, the system worked correctly.

Esta respuesta combina:

- Past Continuous para presentar el contexto.
    
- Past Simple para describir los acontecimientos y las acciones realizadas.
    
- Secuencia cronológica para explicar el resultado.

## 11. A practical guide to choosing the correct tense

Cuando construyas una narración, identifica la función de cada acontecimiento.

|What do you want to communicate?|Tense|
|---|---|
|Una acción terminada|Past Simple|
|Un acontecimiento puntual|Past Simple|
|Una secuencia de acciones|Past Simple|
|Una actividad en desarrollo|Past Continuous|
|El contexto de una historia|Past Continuous|
|Una acción interrumpida|Past Continuous + Past Simple|
|Dos acciones simultáneas|Past Continuous + Past Continuous|

### Decision process

**Step 1. Identify the event.**

¿Estás contando algo que sucedió y terminó? Utiliza Past Simple.

**Step 2. Identify the background activity.**

¿Quieres explicar qué estaba ocurriendo en ese momento? Utiliza Past Continuous.

**Step 3. Connect the events.**

- Usa _when_ para introducir un acontecimiento o momento.
    
- Usa _while_ para destacar una actividad en desarrollo o la simultaneidad.
    

**Step 4. Check the structure.**

- Past Simple: verb in past form.
    
- Past Continuous: was/were + verb-ing.

## 12. Complete narrative example

### A problem during a software project

**English:**

Last Monday, our team was working on a software project. I was reviewing the database while another developer was testing a new feature. Suddenly, the application stopped responding. We checked the system logs and found an error in the database connection. I corrected the configuration, and the application started working again. After that, we tested the feature and completed the task.

**Español:**

El lunes pasado, nuestro equipo estaba trabajando en un proyecto de software. Yo estaba revisando la base de datos mientras otro desarrollador estaba probando una nueva función. Repentinamente, la aplicación dejó de responder. Revisamos los registros del sistema y encontramos un error en la conexión de la base de datos. Corregí la configuración y la aplicación comenzó a funcionar nuevamente. Después de eso, probamos la función y completamos la tarea.

### Grammatical breakdown

|Expression|Tense|Function|
|---|---|---|
|Last Monday|Time expression|Ubicación temporal|
|Our team was working|Past Continuous|Contexto|
|I was reviewing|Past Continuous|Actividad en desarrollo|
|Another developer was testing|Past Continuous|Acción simultánea|
|The application stopped|Past Simple|Acontecimiento|
|We checked|Past Simple|Acción realizada|
|We found|Past Simple|Descubrimiento|
|I corrected|Past Simple|Acción terminada|
|The application started|Past Simple|Resultado|
|We tested|Past Simple|Acción realizada|
|We completed|Past Simple|Finalización|

## 14. Quick reference

|Feature|Past Simple|Past Continuous|
|---|---|---|
|Main function|Acontecimiento pasado|Acción en desarrollo|
|Affirmative|I worked.|I was working.|
|Negative|I didn't work.|I wasn't working.|
|Question|Did you work?|Were you working?|
|Auxiliary|Did|Was / Were|
|Main verb|Past form|Verb-ing|
|Interruption|The phone rang.|I was working.|
|Simultaneous actions|—|I was working while she was studying.|
|Sequence|I arrived and called him.|—|

## 15. Final grammar principles

1. Past Simple presents an event as a past fact, generally completed.
    
2. Past Continuous describes an activity in progress at a past moment.
    
3. Past Continuous frequently provides background information in a narrative.
    
4. Past Simple frequently introduces the events that happen during that background activity.
    
5. _When_ commonly introduces an event or moment.
    
6. _While_ commonly connects activities that were happening simultaneously.
    
7. Both tenses can appear in the same sentence.
    
8. The duration of an action does not automatically determine which tense to use.
    
9. Stative verbs normally remain in Past Simple.
    
10. Combining both tenses allows you to explain technical incidents, professional experiences and everyday events more clearly.
