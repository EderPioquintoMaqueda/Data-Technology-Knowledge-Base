# Past Simple

## 1. Introduction

El **Past Simple** es el tiempo verbal que utilizamos para hablar de acciones, acontecimientos y situaciones que ocurrieron y terminaron en el pasado.

En español suele corresponder al pretérito, como:

- Trabajé.
    
- Estudié.
    
- Desarrollé.
    
- Compré.
    
- Viví.
    
- Fui.
    
- Tuve.
    

En inglés, el Past Simple permite explicar qué hicimos, qué sucedió, dónde estuvimos y cómo se desarrolló una situación en un momento anterior.

Ejemplos:

|English|Español|
|---|---|
|I worked yesterday.|Trabajé ayer.|
|She studied engineering.|Ella estudió ingeniería.|
|We developed an application.|Desarrollamos una aplicación.|
|They visited the factory.|Ellos visitaron la fábrica.|
|He bought a new computer.|Él compró una computadora nueva.|

La característica principal es que la acción se presenta como terminada.

**Concepto clave:** Past Simple = completed actions or situations in the past.

---

## 2. When do we use Past Simple?

### 2.1 Completed actions — acciones terminadas

Se utiliza para expresar acciones que comenzaron y terminaron en el pasado.

- I finished my project.
    
- She sent the report.
    
- We installed the software.
    
- They repaired the machine.
    

En estos ejemplos, las acciones ya concluyeron.

### 2.2 Specific moments in the past — momentos específicos

Cuando sabemos o mencionamos cuándo sucedió una acción.

- I graduated in 2026.
    
- She started her job last year.
    
- We moved to another office in October.
    
- He called me yesterday.
    

### 2.3 Past routines and habits — hábitos del pasado

También describe actividades que realizábamos habitualmente, aunque ya no necesariamente las hacemos.

- I walked to school every day.
    
- She worked in a manufacturing company.
    
- We played football after work.
    
- He studied English when he was younger.
    

### 2.4 Sequences of past events — secuencias de acontecimientos

Se utiliza para narrar acciones que sucedieron una después de otra.

- I opened the application, entered the information and generated the report.
    
- She arrived at the office, checked her emails and attended a meeting.
    
- We inspected the equipment, identified the problem and replaced the component.
    

Es especialmente útil para describir procedimientos y experiencias profesionales.

### 2.5 Past states — estados anteriores

Puede describir situaciones, condiciones o circunstancias que existieron en el pasado.

- I lived in another city.
    
- She was very busy.
    
- The company had three offices.
    
- The system was unstable.

## 3. The two types of past verbs

Para construir el Past Simple es necesario conocer dos categorías fundamentales:

1. **Regular verbs:** forman el pasado generalmente agregando `-ed`.
    
2. **Irregular verbs:** cambian de forma y deben aprenderse individualmente.
    

### 3.1 Regular verbs

Los verbos regulares forman su pasado agregando `-ed` o `-d`.

|Base form|Past Simple|Español|
|---|---|---|
|Work|Worked|Trabajó|
|Develop|Developed|Desarrolló|
|Play|Played|Jugó|
|Open|Opened|Abrió|
|Start|Started|Comenzó|
|Finish|Finished|Terminó|
|Clean|Cleaned|Limpió|
|Install|Installed|Instaló|
|Analyze|Analyzed|Analizó|
|Improve|Improved|Mejoró|

Ejemplos:

- I worked yesterday.
    
- She developed a new application.
    
- We installed the software.
    
- They improved the production process.
    

### 3.2 Irregular verbs

Los verbos irregulares no siguen una regla general para formar el pasado.

|Base form|Past Simple|Español|
|---|---|---|
|Go|Went|Ir|
|Have|Had|Tener|
|Do|Did|Hacer|
|Make|Made|Hacer / fabricar|
|Get|Got|Obtener|
|Take|Took|Tomar / llevar|
|See|Saw|Ver|
|Write|Wrote|Escribir|
|Read|Read|Leer|
|Speak|Spoke|Hablar|
|Buy|Bought|Comprar|
|Bring|Brought|Traer|
|Find|Found|Encontrar|
|Know|Knew|Saber / conocer|
|Think|Thought|Pensar|
|Give|Gave|Dar|
|Meet|Met|Conocer / reunirse|
|Build|Built|Construir|
|Begin|Began|Comenzar|
|Leave|Left|Salir / dejar|

La forma escrita de _read_ es igual en presente y pasado, pero cambia la pronunciación:

- Present: /riːd/
    
- Past: /red/
    

Por eso es importante aprender los verbos irregulares considerando tanto su escritura como su pronunciación.

## 4. Affirmative sentences

### 4.1 Structure

La estructura afirmativa del Past Simple es:

**Subject + verb in past form + complement**

|Subject|Past verb|Complement|
|---|---|---|
|I|worked|yesterday.|
|She|studied|English.|
|We|developed|an application.|
|They|visited|the factory.|

Ejemplos:

- I worked yesterday.
    
- You studied English.
    
- He bought a computer.
    
- She wrote a report.
    
- We developed a database.
    
- They completed the project.
    

### 4.2 Does the verb change depending on the subject?

No. A diferencia del Present Simple, el Past Simple no agrega una terminación especial para la tercera persona singular.

|Subject|Present Simple|Past Simple|
|---|---|---|
|I|Work|Worked|
|You|Work|Worked|
|He|Works|Worked|
|She|Works|Worked|
|We|Work|Worked|
|They|Work|Worked|

Observa:

- I worked.
    
- She worked.
    
- They worked.
    

El verbo permanece en pasado sin importar el sujeto.

**Importante:** no debes agregar `-s` al verbo en pasado.

Incorrect: She worksed yesterday.

Correct: She worked yesterday.

## 5. Spelling rules for regular verbs

Aunque la regla general consiste en agregar `-ed`, existen algunos cambios ortográficos importantes.

### 5.1 General rule: add -ed

La mayoría de los verbos simplemente agrega `-ed`.

|Base form|Past Simple|
|---|---|
|Work|Worked|
|Clean|Cleaned|
|Open|Opened|
|Start|Started|
|Help|Helped|

### 5.2 Verbs ending in -e: add -d

Si el verbo termina en `-e`, solamente se agrega `-d`.

|Base form|Past Simple|
|---|---|
|Live|Lived|
|Move|Moved|
|Like|Liked|
|Change|Changed|
|Use|Used|

### 5.3 Consonant + y: change y to ied

Cuando un verbo termina en consonante + `y`, se cambia la `y` por `i` y se agrega `-ed`.

|Base form|Past Simple|
|---|---|
|Study|Studied|
|Try|Tried|
|Carry|Carried|
|Apply|Applied|

Ejemplo:

- I studied engineering.
    

### 5.4 Vowel + y: add -ed

Si antes de la `y` hay una vocal, se conserva la `y`.

|Base form|Past Simple|
|---|---|
|Play|Played|
|Enjoy|Enjoyed|
|Stay|Stayed|
|Employ|Employed|

### 5.5 Short verbs: doubling the final consonant

Algunos verbos cortos duplican su consonante final antes de agregar `-ed`.

|Base form|Past Simple|
|---|---|
|Stop|Stopped|
|Plan|Planned|
|Shop|Shopped|

La duplicación depende de la estructura y del acento del verbo. No se duplican todas las consonantes finales.

Ejemplo:

- We stopped the machine.
    
- They planned the project.
    

### 5.6 Verbs ending in -c

Algunos verbos terminados en `-c` agregan `-ked`.

|Base form|Past Simple|
|---|---|
|Panic|Panicked|
|Picnic|Picnicked|
|Mimic|Mimicked|

## 6. Negative sentences

Para construir oraciones negativas en Past Simple con verbos de acción, utilizamos el auxiliar **did not** o su contracción **didn't**.

### 6.1 Structure

**Subject + did not + base form of the verb + complement**

|Subject|Auxiliary|Base verb|Complement|
|---|---|---|---|
|I|didn't|work|yesterday.|
|She|didn't|study|English.|
|We|didn't|finish|the project.|
|They|didn't|buy|the equipment.|

Ejemplos:

- I didn't work yesterday.
    
- She didn't study last night.
    
- We didn't finish the project.
    
- They didn't install the software.
    
- He didn't understand the instructions.
    

### 6.2 Why does the verb return to its base form?

Esta es una de las reglas más importantes del Past Simple.

Cuando utilizamos `did` o `didn't`, el auxiliar ya expresa el pasado. Por lo tanto, el verbo principal debe regresar a su forma base.

|Incorrect|Correct|
|---|---|
|I didn't worked.|I didn't work.|
|She didn't studied.|She didn't study.|
|They didn't went.|They didn't go.|
|He didn't bought it.|He didn't buy it.|

**Remember:** did + base form.

---

## 7. Interrogative sentences

Para formular preguntas en Past Simple utilizamos el auxiliar **did** al inicio de la oración.

### 7.1 Structure

**Did + subject + base form of the verb + complement?**

|Auxiliary|Subject|Base verb|Complement|
|---|---|---|---|
|Did|you|work|yesterday?|
|Did|she|study|English?|
|Did|they|finish|the project?|
|Did|he|buy|the computer?|

Ejemplos:

- Did you work yesterday?
    
- Did she study engineering?
    
- Did they finish the project?
    
- Did he call the manager?
    
- Did you understand the instructions?
    

### 7.2 Short answers

Las respuestas cortas utilizan `did` o `didn't`.

|Question|Affirmative|Negative|
|---|---|---|
|Did you work yesterday?|Yes, I did.|No, I didn't.|
|Did she study English?|Yes, she did.|No, she didn't.|
|Did they finish the project?|Yes, they did.|No, they didn't.|
|Did he call you?|Yes, he did.|No, he didn't.|

No es necesario repetir el verbo principal en una respuesta corta.

### 7.3 Wh- questions

Las preguntas con palabras interrogativas utilizan esta estructura:

**Wh-word + did + subject + base verb + complement?**

|Wh-word|Meaning|Example|
|---|---|---|
|What|Qué|What did you do?|
|Where|Dónde|Where did you work?|
|When|Cuándo|When did she arrive?|
|Why|Por qué|Why did they leave?|
|Who|Quién|Who did you meet?|
|How|Cómo|How did you solve the problem?|
|Which|Cuál|Which software did you use?|
|How much|Cuánto|How much did it cost?|
|How many|Cuántos|How many units did you inspect?|

Ejemplos profesionales:

- What did you develop?
    
- Where did you study engineering?
    
- When did you finish the project?
    
- How did you solve the technical problem?
    
- Which programming language did you use?
    
- How many machines did you inspect?
    

**Excepción importante: preguntas cuyo sujeto es Who**

Cuando _who_ pregunta directamente quién realizó la acción, normalmente no utilizamos `did`.

- Who called you? = ¿Quién te llamó?
    
- Who developed the application? = ¿Quién desarrolló la aplicación?
    
- Who managed the project? = ¿Quién administró el proyecto?
    

Compara:

- Who did you call? = ¿A quién llamaste?
    
- Who called you? = ¿Quién te llamó?
    

En la primera pregunta, _who_ es el objeto de la acción. En la segunda, es el sujeto.

## 8. The verb TO BE in Past Simple

El verbo _to be_ es una excepción importante porque no utiliza `did` para formar sus preguntas y negativas.

Sus formas en pasado son:

- **Was:** I, he, she, it.
    
- **Were:** you, we, they.
    

### 8.1 Affirmative

**Subject + was/were + complement**

|Subject|Past form|
|---|---|
|I|was|
|You|were|
|He|was|
|She|was|
|It|was|
|We|were|
|They|were|

Ejemplos:

- I was tired.
    
- She was at the office.
    
- He was an engineer.
    
- We were busy.
    
- They were in the meeting.
    

### 8.2 Negative

**Subject + was/were + not + complement**

|Full form|Contraction|
|---|---|
|Was not|Wasn't|
|Were not|Weren't|

Ejemplos:

- I wasn't at work yesterday.
    
- She wasn't available.
    
- We weren't ready.
    
- They weren't at the factory.
    

### 8.3 Interrogative

**Was/Were + subject + complement?**

Ejemplos:

- Was she at the office?
    
- Were you busy yesterday?
    
- Was the machine operational?
    
- Were they satisfied with the results?
    

### 8.4 Short answers

- Was he there? Yes, he was. / No, he wasn't.
    
- Were you tired? Yes, I was. / No, I wasn't.
    
- Were they ready? Yes, they were. / No, they weren't.
    

### 8.5 Comparison: Past Simple with action verbs vs. to be

|Type|Affirmative|Negative|Question|
|---|---|---|---|
|Action verb|I worked.|I didn't work.|Did I work?|
|To be|I was busy.|I wasn't busy.|Was I busy?|

**Never combine did with was/were to form ordinary Past Simple questions or negatives.**

Incorrect: Did you were at work?

Correct: Were you at work?

Incorrect: I didn't was busy.

Correct: I wasn't busy.

## 9. Time expressions

Las expresiones de tiempo ayudan a identificar que estamos hablando del pasado.

|Expression|Meaning|
|---|---|
|Yesterday|Ayer|
|Yesterday morning|Ayer por la mañana|
|Last night|Anoche|
|Last week|La semana pasada|
|Last month|El mes pasado|
|Last year|El año pasado|
|Last Monday|El lunes pasado|
|Two days ago|Hace dos días|
|Three weeks ago|Hace tres semanas|
|A month ago|Hace un mes|
|In 2024|En 2024|
|In October|En octubre|
|When I was younger|Cuando era más joven|
|A long time ago|Hace mucho tiempo|
|Previously|Anteriormente|
|Earlier|Antes / más temprano|
|Once|Una vez|
|In the past|En el pasado|

Ejemplos:

- I graduated last year.
    
- She called me yesterday.
    
- We completed the project two months ago.
    
- They moved to another office in October.
    
- I studied English when I was younger.
    

### 9.1 The position of time expressions

Las expresiones de tiempo pueden aparecer al principio o al final de una oración.

- Yesterday, I finished the report.
    
- I finished the report yesterday.
    

Ambas son correctas.

En una conversación normal, frecuentemente se colocan al final.

### 9.2 The expression AGO

_Ago_ significa hace y se utiliza para indicar cuánto tiempo ha transcurrido desde un acontecimiento pasado.

Estructura:

**Period of time + ago**

Ejemplos:

- Two days ago.
    
- Three months ago.
    
- Five years ago.
    

En una oración:

- I started learning Python two months ago.
    
- She graduated three years ago.
    
- We installed the equipment a week ago.
    

No confundas:

- I worked two days ago. = Trabajé hace dos días.
    
- I worked for two days. = Trabajé durante dos días.

## 10. Pronunciation of regular verbs ending in -ed

La terminación `-ed` tiene tres pronunciaciones principales. No siempre se pronuncia como una sílaba adicional.

### 10.1 /t/

Se utiliza después de sonidos sordos, excepto /t/.

|Verb|Pronunciation|
|---|---|
|Worked|/wɝːkt/|
|Helped|/helpt/|
|Finished|/ˈfɪnɪʃt/|
|Watched|/wɑːtʃt/|
|Stopped|/stɑːpt/|

### 10.2 /d/

Se utiliza después de sonidos sonoros, excepto /d/.

|Verb|Pronunciation|
|---|---|
|Played|/pleɪd/|
|Opened|/ˈoʊpənd/|
|Cleaned|/kliːnd/|
|Called|/kɔːld/|
|Improved|/ɪmˈpruːvd/|

### 10.3 /ɪd/

Se utiliza cuando el verbo termina en los sonidos /t/ o /d/.

|Verb|Pronunciation|
|---|---|
|Wanted|/ˈwɑːntɪd/|
|Needed|/ˈniːdɪd/|
|Started|/ˈstɑːrtɪd/|
|Decided|/dɪˈsaɪdɪd/|
|Installed|/ɪnˈstɔːld/|

Observa que _installed_ termina fonéticamente en /d/, por lo que su terminación `-ed` se pronuncia /d/, no /ɪd/.

**Important:** la pronunciación depende del último sonido del verbo, no de la última letra escrita.

## 12. Past Simple in professional contexts

El Past Simple es indispensable para explicar experiencias anteriores, resultados, proyectos y responsabilidades que ya concluyeron.

### 12.1 Work experience

- I worked in a mechanical engineering company.
    
- I managed operational information.
    
- I coordinated maintenance activities.
    
- I prepared technical reports.
    
- I collaborated with different departments.
    

### 12.2 Projects and technology

- I developed an internal vehicle management application.
    
- I designed a database using PostgreSQL.
    
- I created a web interface.
    
- I implemented search and reporting functions.
    
- I tested the application with operational data.
    

### 12.3 Engineering

- I repaired the CNC machine.
    
- We replaced the electrical components.
    
- The team installed a new control system.
    
- I designed mechanical components using CAD software.
    
- We identified the source of the equipment failure.
    

### 12.4 Job interviews

En entrevistas laborales, el Past Simple sirve para hablar de logros y experiencias concretas.

**Question: What did you do in your previous position?**

- I managed operational information.
    
- I prepared reports using Excel.
    
- I coordinated maintenance activities.
    
- I developed an internal application to improve information management.
    

**Question: Tell me about a project you completed.**

- I developed a vehicle management application.
    
- I designed the database structure.
    
- I implemented the main functions.
    
- I tested the application and improved its usability.
    

**Question: How did you solve a technical problem?**

- I identified the main issue.
    
- I analyzed the available information.
    
- I evaluated different solutions.
    
- I implemented the most suitable solution.

## 13. Common mistakes made by Spanish speakers

|Incorrect|Correct|Explanation|
|---|---|---|
|I work yesterday.|I worked yesterday.|Una acción terminada requiere pasado.|
|She didn't worked.|She didn't work.|Después de didn't se usa la forma base.|
|Did you went?|Did you go?|Después de did se usa la forma base.|
|He buyed a computer.|He bought a computer.|Buy es irregular.|
|I was work yesterday.|I worked yesterday.|No se utiliza was para formar el Past Simple de un verbo de acción.|
|Did she was there?|Was she there?|To be forma preguntas sin did.|
|They was busy.|They were busy.|They utiliza were.|
|I didn't went there.|I didn't go there.|Did ya expresa el pasado.|
|We studyed English.|We studied English.|Study cambia y por ied.|
|I have 25 years.|I was 25 years old.|Para edad en el pasado se utiliza was/were.|

### Important distinction: I was vs I did

- I was at the office. = Estuve en la oficina.
    
- I worked at the office. = Trabajé en la oficina.
    
- I did the work. = Hice el trabajo.
    

_Was_, _worked_ y _did_ no son intercambiables.

## 14. Past Simple vs Present Simple

Es importante diferenciar una acción habitual actual de una acción terminada en el pasado.

|Present Simple|Past Simple|
|---|---|
|I work in technology.|I worked in manufacturing.|
|She studies English.|She studied French.|
|We use PostgreSQL.|We used MySQL.|
|He lives in Mexico.|He lived in another city.|
|They manage the project.|They managed the project.|

### Structural comparison

|Sentence type|Present Simple|Past Simple|
|---|---|---|
|Affirmative|I work.|I worked.|
|Negative|I don't work.|I didn't work.|
|Question|Do you work?|Did you work?|
|Third person|She works.|She worked.|

Observa que Present Simple utiliza _do/does_, mientras que Past Simple utiliza _did_.

---

## 15. Important distinction: Past Simple vs Present Perfect

Aunque ambos tiempos pueden referirse a acontecimientos anteriores, no comunican exactamente lo mismo.

Por ahora, recuerda esta diferencia general:

- **Past Simple:** presenta una acción como terminada en un momento o periodo pasado.
    
- **Present Perfect:** conecta una experiencia o acción anterior con el presente.
    

Ejemplos:

- I worked there in 2024. = Trabajé ahí en 2024.
    
- I have worked in engineering. = He trabajado en ingeniería.
    

En la primera oración se especifica un periodo pasado terminado. En la segunda se comunica experiencia acumulada hasta el presente.

Present Perfect se estudiará posteriormente en el módulo de Perfect Tenses.

## 17. Quick reference

|Situation|Structure|Example|
|---|---|---|
|Affirmative|Subject + past verb|I worked.|
|Negative|Subject + didn't + base verb|I didn't work.|
|Question|Did + subject + base verb?|Did you work?|
|WH-question|WH + did + subject + base verb?|Where did you work?|
|To be affirmative|Subject + was/were|I was busy.|
|To be negative|Subject + wasn't/weren't|I wasn't busy.|
|To be question|Was/Were + subject?|Were you busy?|

## 18. Final grammar principles

1. Past Simple describes completed actions and situations in the past.
    
2. Regular verbs generally add `-ed` or `-d`.
    
3. Irregular verbs have their own past forms.
    
4. The affirmative form uses the verb in its past form.
    
5. Negative sentences use `didn't + base verb`.
    
6. Questions use `Did + subject + base verb?`.
    
7. The verb does not change according to the subject.
    
8. The verb _to be_ uses _was_ and _were_.
    
9. Questions and negatives with _was/were_ do not require _did_.
    
10. The pronunciation of `-ed` has three main patterns: /t/, /d/ and /ɪd/.
    
11. Time expressions such as _yesterday_, _last year_ and _two days ago_ commonly accompany Past Simple.
    
12. Past Simple is essential for describing professional experience and completed projects.
