# Past Continuous

## 1. Introduction

El **Past Continuous**, también conocido como _Past Progressive_, es un tiempo verbal que utilizamos para describir acciones que estaban ocurriendo en un momento determinado del pasado.

En español suele equivaler a expresiones como:

- Estaba trabajando.
    
- Estaba estudiando.
    
- Estaban desarrollando.
    
- Estábamos revisando.
    
- Estaba funcionando.
    

Su estructura utiliza el pasado del verbo _to be_ (`was/were`) acompañado de un verbo terminado en `-ing`.

Observa:

|English|Español|
|---|---|
|I was working.|Estaba trabajando.|
|She was studying.|Ella estaba estudiando.|
|We were developing an application.|Estábamos desarrollando una aplicación.|
|They were testing the equipment.|Estaban probando el equipo.|

La diferencia principal respecto al Past Simple es el enfoque:

- **Past Simple:** presenta una acción como un acontecimiento terminado.
    
- **Past Continuous:** presenta una acción como un proceso que estaba en desarrollo en un momento del pasado.
    

Ejemplo:

- I worked yesterday. = Trabajé ayer.
    
- I was working at 10 a.m. yesterday. = Ayer a las 10 de la mañana estaba trabajando.
    

La segunda oración nos permite imaginar la actividad en desarrollo en un momento específico.

---

## 2. When do we use Past Continuous?

### 2.1 Actions in progress at a specific time

Se utiliza para describir una actividad que estaba ocurriendo en un momento concreto del pasado.

Ejemplos:

- I was working at 8 p.m.
    
- She was studying at midnight.
    
- We were having a meeting at 10 a.m.
    
- They were repairing the machine yesterday afternoon.
    

El énfasis no está en cuándo terminó la acción, sino en lo que estaba sucediendo en ese momento.

### 2.2 An action interrupted by another action

Es uno de sus usos más importantes. Permite combinar una actividad en desarrollo con un acontecimiento que ocurrió durante ella.

Ejemplos:

- I was working when the phone rang.
    
- She was studying when her manager called.
    
- We were testing the application when the system crashed.
    
- They were repairing the machine when the power went out.
    

En estas oraciones:

- La acción en desarrollo utiliza Past Continuous.
    
- El acontecimiento que ocurre durante ella normalmente utiliza Past Simple.
    

### 2.3 Two simultaneous actions

Se utiliza para describir dos actividades que estaban sucediendo al mismo tiempo.

Ejemplos:

- I was working while she was studying.
    
- The engineers were inspecting the equipment while the technicians were preparing the tools.
    
- We were analyzing the data while the system was processing the information.
    
- They were talking while I was writing the report.
    

Ambas acciones utilizan Past Continuous porque estaban desarrollándose simultáneamente.

### 2.4 Background situations in stories

Se utiliza para establecer el contexto de una historia o narración.

Ejemplos:

- It was raining, and people were waiting outside.
    
- The engineers were working in the factory.
    
- The machine was running, and the technicians were monitoring its performance.
    
- I was walking home when I saw an accident.
    

Past Continuous describe el escenario, mientras que Past Simple puede introducir los acontecimientos principales.

### 2.5 Temporary situations in the past

También puede describir situaciones temporales que existían durante un periodo anterior.

Ejemplos:

- I was living in another city at that time.
    
- She was working for a technology company in 2024.
    
- We were using an older version of the software.
    
- The company was experiencing financial difficulties.

## 3. Sentence structure

La estructura del Past Continuous es:

**Subject + was/were + verb-ing + complement**

El auxiliar cambia según el sujeto, pero el verbo principal conserva la terminación `-ing`.

### 3.1 Affirmative sentences

|Subject|Auxiliary|Verb-ing|Example|
|---|---|---|---|
|I|was|working|I was working.|
|You|were|working|You were working.|
|He|was|working|He was working.|
|She|was|working|She was working.|
|It|was|working|It was working.|
|We|were|working|We were working.|
|They|were|working|They were working.|

Ejemplos:

- I was reading a document.
    
- You were studying English.
    
- He was developing an application.
    
- She was preparing a report.
    
- It was raining.
    
- We were attending a meeting.
    
- They were repairing the equipment.
    

**Remember:**

- I / He / She / It → was
    
- You / We / They → were
    

### 3.2 Negative sentences

Para formar una oración negativa agregamos `not` después de `was` o `were`.

**Subject + was/were + not + verb-ing + complement**

|Full form|Contraction|
|---|---|
|Was not|Wasn't|
|Were not|Weren't|

Ejemplos:

- I was not working.
    
- She wasn't studying.
    
- We weren't waiting.
    
- They weren't using the system.
    
- He wasn't listening to the instructions.
    

La contracción es muy frecuente en conversaciones cotidianas.

### 3.3 Interrogative sentences

Para formular preguntas colocamos `was` o `were` antes del sujeto.

**Was/Were + subject + verb-ing + complement?**

Ejemplos:

- Was she working?
    
- Were you studying?
    
- Was he using the computer?
    
- Were they repairing the machine?
    
- Were you waiting for the manager?
    

### 3.4 Short answers

Las respuestas cortas utilizan el auxiliar correspondiente.

|Question|Affirmative|Negative|
|---|---|---|
|Were you working?|Yes, I was.|No, I wasn't.|
|Was she studying?|Yes, she was.|No, she wasn't.|
|Were they waiting?|Yes, they were.|No, they weren't.|
|Was he driving?|Yes, he was.|No, he wasn't.|

No se repite el verbo principal en las respuestas cortas.

---

## 4. WH- questions in Past Continuous

Las preguntas con palabras interrogativas utilizan esta estructura:

**WH-word + was/were + subject + verb-ing + complement?**

|WH-word|Meaning|Example|
|---|---|---|
|What|Qué|What were you doing?|
|Where|Dónde|Where were you working?|
|When|Cuándo|When was she studying?|
|Why|Por qué|Why were they waiting?|
|Who|Quién|Who was using the computer?|
|How|Cómo|How were you solving the problem?|
|Which|Cuál|Which system were they testing?|

Ejemplos:

- What were you doing yesterday?
    
- Where was she working?
    
- Why were they waiting outside?
    
- Who was managing the project?
    
- Which machine were you inspecting?
    
- How was the system operating?
    

### 4.1 Asking about activities

Una pregunta muy frecuente es:

**What were you doing?**

Significa: ¿Qué estabas haciendo?

Ejemplos:

- What were you doing at 9 p.m.?
    
- What was he doing when you arrived?
    
- What were they doing during the meeting?
    

La pregunta busca conocer una actividad que estaba en desarrollo en un momento anterior.

## 5. Spelling rules for verbs ending in -ing

El verbo principal debe terminar en `-ing`. Aunque generalmente solo agregamos esta terminación, existen algunas reglas ortográficas.

### 5.1 General rule: add -ing

La mayoría de los verbos simplemente agrega `-ing`.

|Base form|-ing form|
|---|---|
|Work|Working|
|Read|Reading|
|Develop|Developing|
|Study|Studying|
|Play|Playing|
|Talk|Talking|
|Monitor|Monitoring|

### 5.2 Verbs ending in -e

Cuando un verbo termina en una `e` muda, normalmente se elimina antes de agregar `-ing`.

|Base form|-ing form|
|---|---|
|Make|Making|
|Write|Writing|
|Use|Using|
|Drive|Driving|
|Improve|Improving|
|Create|Creating|

Ejemplos:

- She was writing a report.
    
- We were using PostgreSQL.
    
- He was driving to work.
    

Existen excepciones, como _be → being_ y _see → seeing_.

### 5.3 Verbs ending in -ie

La terminación `-ie` cambia a `y` antes de agregar `-ing`.

|Base form|-ing form|
|---|---|
|Lie|Lying|
|Die|Dying|
|Tie|Tying|

Ejemplo:

- The plant was dying.
    

### 5.4 Doubling the final consonant

Algunos verbos duplican su consonante final antes de agregar `-ing`.

|Base form|-ing form|
|---|---|
|Run|Running|
|Sit|Sitting|
|Stop|Stopping|
|Plan|Planning|
|Begin|Beginning|

La duplicación suele ocurrir en verbos de una sílaba con el patrón consonante-vocal-consonante, cuando se cumplen las reglas ortográficas de acentuación.

También puede aparecer en verbos de más de una sílaba cuando la última sílaba está acentuada:

- Begin → beginning.
    
- Prefer → preferring.
    

No se duplican todas las consonantes finales:

- Work → working.
    
- Help → helping.
    
- Open → opening.

## 6. Past Continuous vs Past Simple

Esta es la comparación más importante del tema.

Ambos tiempos hablan del pasado, pero presentan las acciones de forma diferente.

|Past Simple|Past Continuous|
|---|---|
|Acción presentada como acontecimiento terminado.|Acción presentada en desarrollo.|
|I worked.|I was working.|
|She called me.|She was calling me.|
|They repaired the machine.|They were repairing the machine.|

### 6.1 Interrupted actions

Cuando una acción estaba ocurriendo y otra sucedió durante ella, normalmente utilizamos:

**Past Continuous + when + Past Simple**

Ejemplos:

- I was working when the phone rang.
    
- She was studying when I arrived.
    
- We were having lunch when the manager called.
    
- The machine was operating when the power failed.
    

También podemos invertir el orden:

- When the phone rang, I was working.
    
- When I arrived, she was studying.
    

El significado permanece prácticamente igual.

### 6.2 Why do we use these two tenses?

Observa:

**I worked when the phone rang.**

La oración puede ser gramatical, pero no expresa tan claramente que ya estabas trabajando cuando sonó el teléfono.

**I was working when the phone rang.**

Aquí se entiende que trabajar era la actividad en desarrollo y que el teléfono sonó durante ella.

La diferencia es de perspectiva y relación temporal.

### 6.3 The timeline

Imagina esta secuencia:

1. Comienzas a trabajar.
    
2. Mientras trabajas, suena el teléfono.
    
3. Respondes la llamada.
    

|Event|Tense|
|---|---|
|You were working.|Past Continuous|
|The phone rang.|Past Simple|
|You answered the phone.|Past Simple|

Representación conceptual:

`Working ─────────────────────`  
`Phone rang`

La línea representa una actividad en desarrollo. El acontecimiento puntual ocurre durante esa actividad.

## 7. WHEN vs WHILE

_When_ y _while_ son dos conectores importantes para combinar acciones en el pasado.

### 7.1 WHEN

_When_ significa cuando.

Se utiliza frecuentemente para introducir el acontecimiento que ocurre durante otra actividad.

Ejemplos:

- I was sleeping when you called.
    
- She was working when the accident happened.
    
- We were testing the system when it stopped working.
    
- They were having a meeting when the alarm rang.
    

Estructura frecuente:

**Past Continuous + when + Past Simple**

También:

**When + Past Simple, + Past Continuous**

### 7.2 WHILE

_While_ significa mientras.

Se utiliza para destacar que una actividad estaba desarrollándose durante otra.

Ejemplos:

- I was studying while she was working.
    
- He was driving while I was checking the map.
    
- The engineers were working while the technicians were inspecting the equipment.
    
- I was reading the documentation while the application was installing.
    

Estructura frecuente:

**While + Past Continuous, + Past Continuous**

También puede utilizarse con una acción puntual:

- While I was working, the phone rang.
    
- The system crashed while we were testing it.
    

### 7.3 Comparison

|Connector|Meaning|Typical use|
|---|---|---|
|When|Cuando|Introducir un acontecimiento o momento.|
|While|Mientras|Destacar la duración o simultaneidad de una actividad.|

**Importante:** estas son tendencias habituales, no reglas absolutas. _When_ y _while_ pueden utilizarse en estructuras diferentes dependiendo del significado.

## 8. Two simultaneous actions

Past Continuous permite describir dos acciones que estaban ocurriendo al mismo tiempo.

Ejemplos cotidianos:

- I was cooking while my brother was watching television.
    
- She was listening to music while she was studying.
    
- They were talking while we were waiting.
    

Ejemplos profesionales:

- The engineers were inspecting the equipment while the technicians were documenting the results.
    
- We were analyzing the financial data while the system was processing the transactions.
    
- The development team was writing code while the testing team was checking the application.
    
- The manager was presenting the results while the employees were taking notes.
    

En estos casos, ambas acciones se encontraban en desarrollo.

## 9. Time expressions

Algunas expresiones permiten situar la acción en un momento del pasado.

|Expression|Meaning|
|---|---|
|At that moment|En ese momento|
|At that time|En ese momento|
|At 8 a.m. yesterday|Ayer a las 8 a. m.|
|At midnight|A medianoche|
|All morning|Toda la mañana|
|All afternoon|Toda la tarde|
|All evening|Toda la noche|
|While|Mientras|
|When|Cuando|
|During the meeting|Durante la reunión|
|At that point|En ese punto|
|Yesterday afternoon|Ayer por la tarde|

Ejemplos:

- I was working at that time.
    
- They were sleeping at midnight.
    
- We were discussing the project during the meeting.
    
- She was preparing the report all morning.
    

**Nota:** una expresión temporal no obliga por sí sola a utilizar Past Continuous. La elección depende de cómo quieras presentar la acción.

---

## 10. Past Continuous and temporary situations

Past Continuous también puede describir una situación temporal que existía durante un periodo pasado.

Ejemplos:

- I was living in another city in 2024.
    
- She was working at a technology company at that time.
    
- We were using an older database system.
    
- They were developing a new product.
    

Compara:

- I worked in engineering. = Trabajé en ingeniería.
    
- I was working in engineering when the project started. = Estaba trabajando en ingeniería cuando comenzó el proyecto.
    

La primera presenta la experiencia como un hecho pasado. La segunda destaca una situación que estaba en desarrollo cuando ocurrió otro acontecimiento.

## 11. Stative verbs and Past Continuous

Los verbos de estado (_stative verbs_) normalmente no se utilizan en Continuous, incluso cuando hablamos del pasado.

Ejemplos:

|Stative verb|Typical Past Simple form|
|---|---|
|Know|I knew the answer.|
|Believe|She believed him.|
|Understand|We understood the problem.|
|Need|They needed help.|
|Want|I wanted a new computer.|
|Own|He owned a company.|
|Prefer|She preferred Python.|
|Remember|I remembered his name.|

Incorrect: I was knowing the answer.

Correct: I knew the answer.

Incorrect: She was needing help.

Correct: She needed help.

### 11.1 Verbs that can change meaning

Algunos verbos pueden utilizarse en Continuous si expresan una actividad dinámica.

|Verb|State|Dynamic activity|
|---|---|---|
|Think|I thought it was correct.|I was thinking about the problem.|
|Have|I had a computer.|I was having lunch.|
|See|I saw the problem.|I was seeing a client.|
|Feel|I felt tired.|I was feeling the material.|
|Look|She looked tired.|She was looking at the screen.|

La elección depende del significado del verbo, no solamente de su traducción al español.

## 12. Past Continuous in professional contexts

Este tiempo resulta útil para describir qué estaba ocurriendo durante un proyecto, una reunión, una intervención técnica o un proceso de análisis.

### 12.1 Technology and programming

- I was developing an application.
    
- We were configuring the database.
    
- The system was processing information.
    
- The team was testing the new software.
    
- I was reviewing the application when I found an error.
    

### 12.2 Data analysis

- I was analyzing financial information.
    
- We were cleaning the dataset.
    
- The application was generating reports.
    
- The database was receiving new records.
    
- I was checking the results when the system stopped responding.
    

### 12.3 Engineering and manufacturing

- The technicians were inspecting the machine.
    
- The engineers were replacing the electrical components.
    
- The equipment was operating normally.
    
- We were testing the CNC system when the motor stopped.
    
- The team was reviewing the technical documentation.
    

### 12.4 Meetings and workplace communication

- I was attending a meeting when you called.
    
- We were discussing the project.
    
- The manager was explaining the new process.
    
- She was presenting the results.
    
- They were preparing the next report.
    

### 12.5 Job interviews

Pregunta:

**What were you doing in your previous position?**

Respuesta:

- I was managing operational information.
    
- I was preparing reports and reviewing technical documents.
    
- I was coordinating activities related to equipment maintenance.
    
- I was developing an internal application to improve information management.
    

Estas respuestas describen actividades que estaban en desarrollo durante un periodo laboral anterior.

## 13. Common mistakes made by Spanish speakers

|Incorrect|Correct|Explanation|
|---|---|---|
|I was work.|I was working.|Se requiere el verbo terminado en -ing.|
|She were studying.|She was studying.|She utiliza was.|
|They was working.|They were working.|They utiliza were.|
|I working yesterday.|I was working yesterday.|Falta el auxiliar.|
|Did you were working?|Were you working?|Past Continuous utiliza was/were en preguntas.|
|I wasn't worked.|I wasn't working.|No se combina wasn't con el verbo en pasado para formar Past Continuous.|
|He was study.|He was studying.|El verbo necesita -ing.|
|We were work.|We were working.|Falta la terminación -ing.|
|While I worked, he called.|While I was working, he called.|La segunda oración destaca la acción en desarrollo.|

### Common translation problem

En español utilizamos expresiones como:

- Estaba trabajando.
    
- Estaba estudiando.
    
- Estaba leyendo.
    

En inglés no se puede traducir literalmente utilizando solamente el verbo principal.

La estructura necesita obligatoriamente:

**Was/Were + verb-ing**

## 14. Past Simple vs Past Continuous: complete comparison

|Context|Past Simple|Past Continuous|
|---|---|---|
|Completed action|I finished the report.|—|
|Action in progress|—|I was writing the report.|
|Interruption|The phone rang.|I was working.|
|Two simultaneous activities|—|We were talking while they were working.|
|Past event sequence|I arrived and opened the door.|—|
|Background description|The machine stopped.|The machine was operating.|

### Example: a complete situation

Imagina que ayer ocurrió un problema técnico.

**Past Continuous:**

- The engineers were testing the equipment.
    
- The system was processing information.
    
- The technicians were monitoring the machine.
    

**Past Simple:**

- The machine stopped.
    
- The system generated an error.
    
- The manager called the technical team.
    

**Combined narrative:**

The engineers were testing the equipment when the machine stopped. The system generated an error while the technicians were monitoring its performance.

Traducción:

Los ingenieros estaban probando el equipo cuando la máquina se detuvo. El sistema generó un error mientras los técnicos monitoreaban su funcionamiento.

## 15. Quick reference

|Sentence type|Structure|Example|
|---|---|---|
|Affirmative|Subject + was/were + verb-ing|I was working.|
|Negative|Subject + wasn't/weren't + verb-ing|I wasn't working.|
|Question|Was/Were + subject + verb-ing?|Were you working?|
|WH-question|WH + was/were + subject + verb-ing?|What were you doing?|
|Interrupted action|Past Continuous + when + Past Simple|I was working when he called.|
|Simultaneous actions|Past Continuous + while + Past Continuous|I was working while she was studying.|

### Main differences

|Present Continuous|Past Continuous|
|---|---|
|I am working.|I was working.|
|She is studying.|She was studying.|
|They are developing.|They were developing.|
|We are testing the system.|We were testing the system.|

El cambio principal es el auxiliar:

- Present Continuous: am/is/are.
    
- Past Continuous: was/were.

## 17. Final grammar principles

1. Past Continuous describes actions that were in progress at a moment in the past.
    
2. Its structure is `was/were + verb-ing`.
    
3. Use _was_ with I, he, she and it.
    
4. Use _were_ with you, we and they.
    
5. Negative sentences use _wasn't_ or _weren't_.
    
6. Questions begin with _was_ or _were_.
    
7. Use Past Continuous with Past Simple to describe interrupted actions.
    
8. Use _while_ frequently to connect simultaneous activities.
    
9. Use _when_ frequently to introduce an event or moment.
    
10. Stative verbs normally do not appear in Continuous forms.
    
11. Some verbs can be stative or dynamic depending on their meaning.
    
12. Past Continuous is useful for describing workplace activities, technical processes and events in progress.
