# 01 — Sentence Structure and Verb To Be

## 1. Learning objectives

- Understand the basic structure of English sentences.
    
- Identify personal pronouns.
    
- Conjugate the verb _to be_ in the present tense.
    
- Construct affirmative, negative and interrogative sentences.
    
- Understand basic English word order.
    

## 2. Basic sentence structure

**Subject + Verb + Complement**

Example:

- I work here. — Trabajo aquí.
    
- She studies English. — Ella estudia inglés.
    
- We develop software. — Desarrollamos software.
    

English generally requires an explicit subject.

## 4. Verb To Be — Present

|Subject|Verb|Meaning|
|---|---|---|
|I|am|Soy / estoy|
|You|are|Eres / estás|
|He|is|Es / está|
|She|is|Es / está|
|It|is|Es / está|
|We|are|Somos / estamos|
|They|are|Son / están|

### Uses

1. Identity: I am an engineer.
    
2. Profession: She is a manager.
    
3. Emotions: I am happy.
    
4. Location: We are at work.
    
5. Characteristics: The system is reliable.
    

## 5. Sentence forms

### Affirmative

Subject + am/is/are + complement.

- I am ready.
    
- She is a programmer.
    
- They are colleagues.
    

### Negative

Subject + am/is/are + not + complement.

- I am not tired.
    
- She is not here.
    
- They are not ready.
    

### Contractions

|Full form|Contraction|
|---|---|
|I am|I'm|
|You are|You're|
|He is|He's|
|She is|She's|
|It is|It's|
|We are|We're|
|They are|They're|
|Is not|Isn't|
|Are not|Aren't|

### Interrogative

Am/Is/Are + subject + complement?

- Are you ready?
    
- Is she a manager?
    
- Are they at work?
    

### Short answers

- Are you ready? — Yes, I am.
    
- Is she here? — No, she isn't.
    
- Are they colleagues? — Yes, they are.
    

## 6. Important distinction: TO BE vs DO

With the verb TO BE, questions do not require DO or DOES.

Correct: Are you ready?

Incorrect: Do you are ready?

Other verbs use DO/DOES in Present Simple:

- Do you work here?
    
- Does she speak English?

## 9. Exercises

1. Write five affirmative sentences using TO BE.
    
2. Transform those sentences into negative form.
    
3. Transform them into questions.
    
4. Answer five questions using short answers.
    
5. Write a short personal introduction in English.
    

## 10. Speaking challenge

Speak for one minute about yourself.

Include:

- Your name.
    
- Your profession.
    
- Your workplace.
    
- Your interests.
    
- Your current English learning goals.
    

## 11. Review checklist

- I understand English sentence structure.
    
- I can identify all personal pronouns.
    
- I know when to use AM, IS and ARE.
    
- I can construct affirmative sentences.
    
- I can construct negative sentences.
    
- I can ask questions using TO BE.
    
- I can pronounce the basic forms.
