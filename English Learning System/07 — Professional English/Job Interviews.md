# 🎤 Job Interviews in English (Data & Tech)

## 1. Structure of a tech interview
1. **Recruiter screen** (15–30 min) — background, motivation, salary, English level.
2. **Hiring manager** — experience, projects, behavioural questions.
3. **Technical** — SQL/Python live coding, case study, take-home.
4. **Panel / culture fit** — teamwork, communication.
5. **Offer** — negotiation.

## 2. "Tell me about yourself" — formula: Present → Past → Future
> *"I'm a **mechanical engineer turned data analyst**. **Currently**, I work at [company], where I build Power BI dashboards and automate reports with Python and SQL. **Before that**, I spent five years in engineering, **which gave me** strong analytical and problem-solving skills. **Recently** I've been focusing on data engineering — I've built ETL pipelines with PostgreSQL and Airflow. **I'm now looking for** a role where I can **combine** my engineering background **with** data, and that's why this position caught my attention."*

## 3. The STAR method for behavioural questions
| Letter | Meaning | Useful phrases |
|---|---|---|
| **S**ituation | context | *At my previous company, we had a problem with…* |
| **T**ask | your responsibility | *I was responsible for… / My goal was to…* |
| **A**ction | what **you** did (use "I", not "we") | *First, I analysed… Then I proposed… I implemented…* |
| **R**esult | measurable outcome | *As a result, report time **dropped from** 3 days **to** 2 hours.* |

### Example — "Tell me about a time you solved a difficult problem"
> *(S) Our monthly sales report took three days to prepare manually in Excel. (T) I was asked to speed it up. (A) I mapped the data sources, wrote SQL queries to consolidate them in PostgreSQL, and built a Python script that refreshed a Power BI dashboard automatically every morning. (R) The report now takes **about ten minutes**, errors **dropped by 90 percent**, and the team saves **around 20 hours a month**.*

## 4. Common questions and starter answers
| Question | Starter |
|---|---|
| Why do you want to work here? | *I've been following your work on…, and I'm excited about…* |
| What are your strengths? | *One of my main strengths is… For example, …* |
| What is your biggest weakness? | *I used to… so I've been working on it by… Now I…* |
| Where do you see yourself in five years? | *I see myself growing into a … role, leading…* |
| Why are you leaving your current job? | *I've learned a lot, but I'm looking for more challenges in…* |
| Tell me about a conflict with a colleague. | *We disagreed on… I listened to… We agreed to…* |
| Tell me about a failure. | *A project that didn't go as planned was… What I learned was…* |
| How do you prioritise? | *I consider impact and urgency… I communicate trade-offs…* |
| What are your salary expectations? | *Based on my research and experience, I'm looking for a range of… but I'm open to discussing the full package.* |
| Do you have any questions for us? | *Yes — what does success look like in the first 90 days? / How is the data team structured? / What's your tech stack?* |

## 5. Technical interview phrases (think aloud!)
| Situation | Phrase |
|---|---|
| Clarify | *Just to clarify, do you want … or …?* / *Can I assume the IDs are unique?* |
| Plan | *My approach would be to first…, then…* |
| Buy time | *That's a good question — let me think about it for a second.* |
| Explain choice | *I'm choosing a LEFT JOIN because…* |
| Trade-offs | *This is simpler, but it wouldn't scale to billions of rows.* |
| Stuck | *I'm not sure about the exact syntax, but the logic would be…* |
| Test | *Let me walk through an example to check it.* |
| Complexity | *This runs in linear time, O(n).* |
| Improve | *If I had more time, I would add tests and handle nulls.* |
