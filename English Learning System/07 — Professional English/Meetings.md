# 🤝 Meetings in English

## 1. Opening
- *Hi everyone, thanks for joining. Shall we get started?*
- *Let's wait a couple more minutes for the others to join.*
- *The goal of today's meeting is to decide…*
- *We have three items on the agenda.*

## 2. Daily stand-up template
> *Yesterday I **worked on** the customer churn query. Today I'**m going to** finish the dashboard and **start** testing. **I'm blocked by** missing access to the production database — could someone help me with that?*

| Section | Phrase |
|---|---|
| Done | *Yesterday I finished / completed / fixed…* |
| Plan | *Today I'll focus on… / I'm planning to…* |
| Blockers | *I'm blocked by… / I need help with… / No blockers.* |

## 3. Giving opinions
| Strength | Phrase |
|---|---|
| Neutral | *I think… / In my opinion… / From my point of view…* |
| Tentative | *I might be wrong, but… / It seems to me that…* |
| Strong | *I'm convinced that… / I strongly believe…* |

## 4. Agreeing and disagreeing politely
| Agree | Disagree (softly) |
|---|---|
| *I completely agree.* | *I see your point, but…* |
| *That's a good point.* | *I'm not sure I agree with that.* |
| *Exactly. / Absolutely.* | *That's true, however we should also consider…* |
| *I'm with you on that.* | *I'd look at it differently.* |
| *That makes sense.* | *Could we look at the data before deciding?* |

## 5. Interrupting, clarifying and checking
| Purpose | Phrase |
|---|---|
| Interrupt | *Sorry to interrupt, but… / Can I jump in here?* |
| Ask to repeat | *Sorry, could you repeat that? / You broke up a bit.* |
| Clarify | *What do you mean by…? / Just to make sure I understand, you're saying…?* |
| Slow down | *Could you speak a bit more slowly, please?* |
| Check understanding | *Does that make sense? / Is that clear?* |
| Bring back on topic | *Let's get back to the main point.* |
| Postpone | *Let's take this offline. / Let's park that for now.* |

## 6. Online meeting problems
- *Can you hear me? / You're on mute.*
- *I think your connection is unstable.*
- *Can you see my screen? / Let me share my screen.*
- *I'll drop the link in the chat.*
- *Sorry, I had connection issues — what did I miss?*

## 7. Closing and actions
- *Let's wrap up. To sum up, we agreed that…*
- *The action items are: Ana will…, Luis will… by Friday.*
- *Who's going to take care of…?*
- *I'll send a follow-up email with the notes.*
- *Thanks, everyone. Have a good one!*

### Follow-up email template
> **Subject:** Follow-up: Data quality meeting – Nov 18
> Hi team,
> Thanks for today's meeting. Here's a quick summary:
> **Decisions:** We'll move the nightly job to 2 a.m.
> **Action items:** Ana – add null checks (Fri) · Luis – update the dashboard (Mon)
> **Next meeting:** Tuesday, 10 a.m.
> Let me know if I missed anything.
> Best, Eder
