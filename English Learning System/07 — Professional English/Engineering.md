## 3. Useful sentences
- *The vibration readings **exceeded the threshold** three times last week.*
- *We **ran a root cause analysis** and found that the bearing **was worn out**.*
- *Predictive maintenance **reduced unplanned downtime by** 25 percent.*
- *The sensor **samples** temperature **every** five seconds.*
- *The part **failed** the quality inspection **due to** dimensional tolerances.*

## 4. Selling your background (interview)
> *"My mechanical engineering background **taught me to** think in systems, measure everything and **solve problems methodically**. **That's exactly what** data work requires: understanding the process **behind** the numbers."*
