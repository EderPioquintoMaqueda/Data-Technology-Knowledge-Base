# 🎛️ Modal Verbs — Overview

Modals add **ability, possibility, obligation, advice or permission**. Rules: no "-s" in 3rd person (*she can*), followed by the **base verb** without "to" (*can run*, not *can to run*), questions by inversion (*Can you…?*).

| Modal | Function | Example | Español | Detailed note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| can | ability / permission | I **can** write SQL. **Can** I join? | poder / saber | [[Can and Could]] |
| could | past ability / polite request / possibility | **Could** you review my PR? It **could** be the cache. | podría | [[Can and Could]] |
| may | formal permission / possibility | **May** I ask a question? It **may** rain. | puede que | [[May and Might]] |
| might | weaker possibility | The job **might** fail. | quizás | [[May and Might]] |
| must | strong obligation (internal) / deduction | You **must** use 2FA. It **must** be a typo. | deber / seguro que | [[Must and Have To]] |
| have to | obligation (external rule) | I **have to** submit it by Friday. | tener que | [[Must and Have To]] |
| don't have to | no obligation | You **don't have to** come. | no es necesario | [[Must and Have To]] |
| mustn't | prohibition | You **mustn't** share passwords. | no debes | [[Must and Have To]] |
| should / ought to | advice | You **should** add tests. | deberías | [[Should and Ought To]] |
| would | polite / hypothetical | **Would** you like coffee? I **would** use Polars. | -ría | [[Second Conditional]] |
| will | future / promise | I **will** send it today. | -ré | [[Future with Will]] |
| shall | offer / suggestion (UK, formal) | **Shall** we start? | ¿empezamos? | — |

## Modal perfects (past speculation & regret)
| Structure | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| must have + PP | almost sure (past) | The server **must have restarted**. |
| might / could have + PP | possible (past) | Someone **might have deleted** the file. |
| can't have + PP | impossible (past) | He **can't have finished** already. |
| should have + PP | regret / criticism | We **should have tested** it. |
| would have + PP | unreal past | I **would have helped** if you'd asked. |

## Politeness scale for requests
*Send me the file.* (direct) → *Can you send me the file?* → *Could you send me the file?* → *Would you mind sending me the file?* → *I was wondering if you could send me the file.* (very polite)
