# 🇬🇧 English Learning System — Dashboard

> **Strategy:** learn English **through** your technical work. Every Python, SQL and tools note in this vault is written in English and ends with a **vocabulary table (IPA + Spanish-style pronunciation + translation)**. This system adds the grammar, vocabulary, numbers and sentence-building you need to go from A2 to C1.
> First read: [[How to Read Pronunciation]]

## 🗓️ Daily routine (60 min)
| Min | Activity | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | Review vocabulary (spaced repetition) | [[Vocabulary Database]] |
| 15 | Grammar or sentence-building lesson + exercises | path below |
| 20 | Study one tech note **reading it aloud** | [[00 - Python Roadmap]] · [[00 - SQL Roadmap]] |
| 10 | Listening / shadowing | [[Pronunciation Drills for Spanish Speakers]] |
| 5 | Speak: record 1 minute about what you studied | [[Speaking Templates and Daily Practice]] |
Sunday: [[Weekly Evaluations]] · End of month: [[Exams]]

## 🛤️ Learning path by level

### 🟢 Stage 1 — Foundations (A1 → A2) · weeks 1–6
1. [[How to Read Pronunciation]] → [[English Pronunciation]]
2. [[Sentence Structure]] (verb *to be*) → [[Personal Pronouns]] → [[Parts of Speech]]
3. [[Articles and Determiners]] → [[Singular and Plural Nouns]] → [[Adjectives and Adverbs]]
4. [[Numbers in English]] ⭐
5. [[Present Simple]] → [[Present Continuous]] → [[Present Simple vs Present Continuous]]
6. [[Past Simple]] + [[Regular Verbs]] + [[Irregular Verbs]] → [[Past Continuous]] → [[Past Simple vs Past Continuous]]
7. Sentence building: [[SB-01 Simple Sentences - Word Order]] → [[SB-02 Questions and Negatives]]
8. Vocabulary: [[Everyday Vocabulary]] · [[False Cognates]]

### 🟡 Stage 2 — Intermediate (A2 → B1) · weeks 7–16
1. Future: [[Future with Will]] → [[Be Going To]] → [[Future with Present Continuous]] → [[Future Simple vs Be Going To vs Present Continuous]] → [[Future Tenses Consolidation]]
2. Perfect: [[Present Perfect]] → [[Present Perfect vs Past Simple]] → [[Present Perfect Continuous]] → [[Present Perfect vs Present Perfect Continuous]]
3. Modals: [[Modal Verbs]] → [[Can and Could]] → [[Must and Have To]] → [[Should and Ought To]] → [[May and Might]]
4. Conditionals: [[Zero Conditional]] → [[First Conditional]]
5. [[Prepositions]] · [[Used to and Would]]
6. Sentence building: [[SB-03 Compound Sentences - FANBOYS]] → [[SB-04 Complex Sentences - Subordinate Clauses]]
7. Vocabulary: [[Collocations]] · [[Phrasal Verbs]] · [[Data and Programming]] ⭐

### 🟠 Stage 3 — Upper-intermediate (B1 → B2) · weeks 17–30
1. [[Past Perfect]] → [[Past Perfect vs Past Simple]] → [[Past Perfect vs Past Continuous]] → [[Past Perfect Continuous]] → [[Past Tenses Consolidation]]
2. [[Present Perfect vs Past Perfect]] → [[Present Perfect Continuous vs Past Perfect Continuous]] → [[Perfect Tenses Consolidation]]
3. [[Future Continuous]] → [[Future Perfect]] → [[Future Perfect Continuous]]
4. [[Second Conditional]] → [[Third Conditional]] → [[Mixed Conditionals]] → [[Conditionals Consolidation]]
5. [[Passive Voice]] · [[Reported Speech]]
6. Sentence building: [[SB-05 Relative Clauses]]
7. Professional: [[Meetings]] · [[Writing Professional Emails and Messages]] · [[Engineering]]

### 🔴 Stage 4 — Advanced (B2 → C1) · weeks 31+
1. [[SB-06 Advanced Structures and Connectors]] (inversion, cleft sentences, participle clauses)
2. [[Uncommon Words]] · [[Idioms and Expressions]]
3. [[Presentations]] · [[Job Interviews]] · [[IBM and Technology]]
4. Write one technical blog post per month in English about a project.
