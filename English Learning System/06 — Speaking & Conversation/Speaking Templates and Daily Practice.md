# 🗣️ Speaking Templates and Daily Practice

## 1. Small talk (first 2 minutes of every call)
| Question | Natural answer |
|---|---|
| How's it going? / How are you doing? | *Pretty good, thanks. Busy week! How about you?* |
| How was your weekend? | *It was great — I went hiking / I just relaxed at home.* |
| Where are you based? | *I'm based in Mexico City.* |
| What time is it there? | *It's 9 a.m. here — we're one hour ahead of you.* |
| Any plans for the holidays? | *Nothing special, probably spending time with family.* |
| How's the weather? | *It's been really hot / rainy lately.* |

## 2. Fillers that give you time to think (instead of "eh…")
*Well… · Let me think… · That's a good question… · The thing is… · Actually… · I mean… · How can I put it… · So, basically…*

## 3. When you don't know a word (circumlocution)
- *It's a kind of… / It's like a… / It's the thing you use to…*
- *I don't know the exact word, but it's…*
- *How do you say "…" in English?*

## 4. Speaking templates
### Explaining a project (2 minutes)
> *The project was about **[goal]**. The problem was that **[pain point]**. I used **[tools]** to **[actions]**. The main challenge was **[challenge]**, which I solved by **[solution]**. In the end, **[result with numbers]**. If I did it again, I would **[improvement]**.*

### Explaining a technical concept simply
> *In simple terms, **[concept]** is **[analogy]**. For example, **[example]**. It's useful because **[benefit]**.*
> *In simple terms, an index is like the index at the back of a book: instead of reading every page, the database jumps straight to the right one. It's useful because queries become much faster.*

### Giving an update
> *Quick update on **[task]**: I've finished **[done]**, I'm currently working on **[in progress]**, and I expect to be done by **[date]**. One risk is **[risk]**.*

### Asking for help
> *Hey **[name]**, do you have a minute? I'm stuck on **[problem]**. I've already tried **[attempts]**. Could you point me in the right direction?*

## 5. 30-day speaking challenge
| Week | Daily task (5–10 min, record yourself) |
|---|---|
| 1 | Describe your day yesterday (past simple). |
| 2 | Explain one technical concept from this vault in simple terms. |
| 3 | Describe a chart or dashboard with numbers ([[Presentations]]). |
| 4 | Answer one interview question with STAR ([[Job Interviews]]). |

## 6. Self-correction checklist (listen to your recording)
- [ ] Did I use the third-person **-s**? (*she works*)
- [ ] Did I pronounce **-ed** correctly? ([[Regular Verbs]])
- [ ] Did I avoid adding "e" before s- words? (*SQL, start*)
- [ ] Did I use the right tense for finished vs unfinished time? (*I worked* vs *I have worked*)
- [ ] Did I use linking words? (*so, because, however*)
- [ ] Was my speed calm and clear?

## 7. Conversation practice options
- Language exchange: Tandem, HelloTalk.
- Paid tutors: italki, Preply, Cambly.
- AI voice practice: ask an assistant to role-play an interviewer or a stand-up meeting and correct your grammar after each answer.
- Join English-speaking tech meetups or Discord communities.
